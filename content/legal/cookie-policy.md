# Cookie Policy

Last updated August 19, 2026. Effective January 18, 2026.

## Introduction

EUANGELION uses a small number of first-party cookies. Essential cookies are set because the Service cannot function without them; anything beyond that is opt-in through the consent banner — and there is currently nothing beyond that. We set no advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking cookies, and no third-party analytics cookies.

## The Cookies We Actually Set

euangelion_session — an anonymous session token that keeps your reading session together. Expires after 30 days.

euangelion_audit_session — the Soul Audit session token that lets the reading paths you generated be reopened. Expires after 30 days, with the anonymous session.

euangelion_site_consent — records your consent choice itself, so the banner does not ask again. Expires after 180 days.

Supabase authentication cookies — set only when you sign in, to keep you signed in securely.

## Browser Storage Beyond Cookies

Your browser also holds local storage (app settings, theme, and reading preferences), IndexedDB (clipped quotes and offline data), and a service worker cache (pages, fonts, artwork, and narration audio for offline reading). These stay on your device and are not transmitted to us.

## Analytics

We do not run third-party analytics. The consent banner records an analytics opt-in flag so that if first-party analytics are ever added, they stay off for anyone who declined. Declining changes nothing about how the Service works.

## Managing Cookies

You can clear or block cookies at any time through your browser's privacy settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge all provide controls under Privacy). Blocking or clearing the essential cookies signs you out and resets anonymous reading state; nothing else breaks.

Clearing the consent cookie makes the banner ask again, which is also how you change your earlier answer.

## Children's Privacy

We do not set non-essential cookies for anyone, including children under 13.

## Updates and Contact

Material changes to this policy are announced by updating the date above and posting a notice on the Service. Questions: privacy@euangelion.app. This policy should be read alongside the Privacy Policy at /privacy and the Terms of Service at /terms.
