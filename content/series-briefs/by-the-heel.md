## One-line theme

The food laws were God's real gift and set a people apart as holy; Christ, the firstborn who died and left a will, gives the inheritance to people who could only come holding his heel, so the freedom that results is received with reverence and spent on love for a brother.

## The emotional center

How people who love each other walk together now that the veil is torn. Christ looks past flesh, money, law and tradition to the person, and the Father wants more for his heirs than any parent wants for a child (Matthew 7:11). Freedom is measured by how it treats the brother it could wound (Romans 14:15, KJV: "now walkest thou not charitably").

## Doctrine guardrail

Scripture calls Christ the Son and "the firstborn among many brothers" (Romans 8:29) and says whoever has seen him has seen the Father (John 14:9). The Father's love for his heirs is presented as revealed in Christ, and Christ as the elder brother who shares the inheritance rather than guarding it (Hebrews 2:11; contrast Luke 15:25-32). The persons of the Trinity are not blurred.
