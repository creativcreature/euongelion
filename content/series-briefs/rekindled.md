## Founder rulings recorded at lock (2026-08-16)

- **Thematic:** REKINDLED. Named for ἀναζωπυρεῖν (_anazōpyrein_, 2 Tim 1:6) — "to
  rekindle." The small group carrying this series is itself named Rekindled.
- **Backbone is the order of operations, not a tactic.** "He would love first" =
  1 John 4:19. Founder: _"because he would love first, we have relationship, that
  is how we should exist, in relationship with Him, and in community."_
- **Governing resolution:** _"the relationship we have with Him dictates everything."_
- **Rekindling relationship with Him and with the Word are one act,** because the
  Word is Him (John 1:1 deliberately echoing Genesis 1:1).
- **Posture:** we come as **lambs**, near a fire we did not light and are not asked
  to feed. Mary and Martha do not appear. Luke 10 is not used.
- **Shape:** mirror, then mercy. Days 1-3 name the dimness honestly; day 4 turns;
  days 5-7 land in mercy and send outward.
- **Bereshit is stated plainly**, in the founder's own words: _"look what sits in
  this word."_ Framed as observation, never as authorial intent.
- **Excluded from the site:** the founder's friend's brother (deathbed turn three
  days before death). Room-only. Day 5 uses Monica and Augustine instead.

## One-line theme

The fire you are trying to get back was lit by Someone else, sworn in fire while
you slept, and rekindling it is tending — never ignition.

## Why this series is not the four adjacent ones

`abiding-in-his-presence` owns John 15 abiding. `too-busy-for-god` owns busyness
via Ecclesiastes. `present-in-the-chaos` owns stillness. `hearing-god-in-the-noise`
owns discernment. All four are **discipline** series — slow down, filter, be still,
dwell. Rekindled is a **covenant and inheritance** series. No existing series on the
site uses fire, lamp, flame, altar, or lamb language. Verified 2026-08-16: zero slug
collisions, zero fire/lamp/flame matches in `src/data/series.ts`.

## 7-day chiastic outline

### Day 1 (A) — Hook: Look What Sits in This Word

- Anchor: Genesis 1:1 (KJV) + John 1:1 (KJV)
- Theme: The Bible's first word, בְּרֵאשִׁית, holds אֵשׁ (_esh_, fire) inside
  בְּרִית (_b'rit_, covenant). John opens with the same three words as Genesis to
  say the Word is a Person. Rekindling your relationship with Him and with the Word
  is therefore one act.
- Meta-story line: CREATION — the beginning, where the fire was already burning
  before anyone was there to see it.
- Handled honestly: the letter pattern is homiletical, not etymological. _Bereshit_
  is ב (in) + ראש (head/beginning). Say "look what sits in this word," never "this
  is what Moses encoded."

### Day 2 (B) — Build: Sworn in Fire While You Slept

- Anchor: Genesis 15:12,17 (KJV)
- Theme: A deep sleep falls on Abram and God passes between the pieces **alone**,
  as a burning lamp. In a covenant of that kind both parties walk the aisle; only
  One walks here. Paired with Mark 4:26-29 — the farmer sleeps and rises and the
  seed grows, "though he knows not how." You were asleep for the part that saved you.
- NT connection: Hebrews 6:13, He swore by Himself.

### Day 3 (C) — Pivot: Fan Into Flame ⭐ (write FIRST)

- Anchor: 2 Timothy 1:6-7 (BSB)
- Theme: ἀναζωπυρεῖν — _ana_ (again) + _zō-_ (alive) + _pyr_ (fire). It appears
  exactly once in the New Testament. Paul writes it to a young man who is afraid,
  mid-transition, whose faith reached him secondhand through his grandmother Lois
  and his mother Eunice (2 Tim 1:5). Paired with Leviticus 6:13: the altar fire is
  never lit twice, only kept.
- The "aha": **Rekindling is not ignition.** The gift is already in you. Nobody is
  being asked to start a fire — only to stop letting one go out. And the group
  reading this is named for a verb Paul used once.

### Day 4 (B-prime) — Apply: Lambs at the Fire

- Anchor: Exodus 29:38-39,42 (KJV)
- Theme: A lamb morning and evening, continually, at the altar whose fire never
  goes out. We come to the fire as lambs — not as its keepers. Isaiah 40:11, He
  gathers the lambs in His arm. John 21:15, "Feed my lambs," spoken to Peter
  **after** Peter failed, which is the whole point.
- This is where the founder's "sitting at his feet" lands: at an altar, as a lamb,
  not as a student at a chair.

### Day 5 (A-prime) — Resolve: Our Lamps Are Going Out

- Anchor: 1 Samuel 3:3 (KJV) + Matthew 25:5,8 (BSB)
- Theme: "The lamp of God had not yet gone out" — and God calls Samuel in the night
  while Eli sleeps and the light is nearly done. All ten virgins sleep; sleep is not
  what separates them. Oil is. Exodus 27:20-21 makes the oil a community supply and
  a statute forever.
- Story: Monica and Augustine (see source pack). Her plan to sail with him failed
  utterly, and that failure is what carried him to Milan and Ambrose.

### Day 6 — Recap: Light First

- The five takeaways integrated, no new exegesis. Closes outward: because He loved
  first, you can light first. Matthew 5:14-16 — a lamp is not lit to go under a
  basket. Isaiah 60:1. Includes the required Further-Your-Learning section.

### Day 7 — Sabbath

- Single anchor: Leviticus 6:13 (KJV), "the fire shall ever be burning upon the
  altar; it shall never go out."
- One-line invitation: sit near it. Nothing to tend today.
