**Length:** Two artifacts — Deep Dive (~3,500–4,500 words master document) + 7 daily readings (~1,500–2,000 words each)

---

## Why John 3 is the spine

John 3:1–21 is the only passage that holds, in 21 verses, **all five themes the founder originally named**: salvation, Jesus' words, "saved & faithing," and baptism of water + Spirit + death. It also carries:

- A built-in **Old Testament typology** — Numbers 21:6–9 (the bronze serpent on the staff) — that Jesus Himself names in 3:14
- Three **explicit Hebrew/Greek pivots** — _gennēthē anōthen_ (γεννηθῇ ἄνωθεν, "born again / from above"), _pneuma_ (πνεῦμα) ↔ _ruach_ (רוּחַ, breath/wind/spirit), _pisteuō_ (πιστεύω, present-active continuous "trusting")
- **John 3:16** — the most-read verse in Christianity — at its center
- A natural **light-vs-darkness** frame (Nicodemus comes by night; "the light has come into the world") that runs through every day of the week

A topical series fragments. A passage immersion deepens. This week immerses.

## Internal daily chiasm (A-B-C-B'-A' inside each daily)

- **A** — Hero + Anchor Scriptures + Quickstart (modern hook → tension surfaced)
- **B** — Reflection + Prayer (textual / relational engagement)
- **C** — STORYTIME + Story (the pivot — concrete narrative; abstract becomes embodied)
- **B'** — Go Deeper + Scripture Reading + Key Word (theological deep dive)
- **A'** — Community Reflection + This Week's Journey + Takeaway + CTA (return to opening, reader transformed)

## PaRDeS (at both levels)

In the deep dive: PaRDeS gets its own H2 section with four sub-headings — _Peshat_ (פשט) — Plain, _Remez_ (רמז) — Hint, _Derash_ (דרש) — Search, _Sod_ (סוד) — Secret.

In each daily: PaRDeS threads silently through section ordering:

- Peshat → Anchor Scriptures + Quickstart
- Remez → Hebrew/Greek word study inside Go Deeper
- Derash → Reflection + Story (application to lived life)
- Sod → the prophetic / typological reading inside Go Deeper (Day 5's bronze serpent → cross typology IS Sod)

## Translation policy

Public-domain / CC0 only — BSB / WEB / WEBBE / KJV / ASV / YLT / DARBY / BBE. Validator continues to reject NIV/ESV/NASB/NRSV/CSB/NLT/MSG. Per-passage translation choices follow the brief's rationale per anchor (e.g., KJV for prophetic poetry like Habakkuk; BSB for Pauline argument; ASV when paired with Greek vocab).

The `/about/translations` page describes this stance publicly; footer links there from every page.

## Hebrew & Greek transliteration discipline (NEW non-negotiable)

Wherever Hebrew or Greek text appears, transliteration MUST appear alongside. Pattern: _gennēthē anōthen_ (γεννηθῇ ἄνωθεν), _ruach_ (רוּחַ). Italic transliteration first OR original-script first — but never one without the other. Validator audits this and flags unpaired occurrences as NEEDS-FIX.

## Story sourcing (NEW non-negotiable)

Every Story section is real, named, citable. Never invented. Mix per founder direction:

- **Recent verifiable news** (Chilean miners style — Day 6 candidate)
- **Historical Christian witnesses** (named saints, missionaries, martyrs with primary-source documentation — e.g., Augustine's mother Monica, Brother Lawrence, Lottie Moon)
- **Documented contemporary testimonies** (citable interviews, books, public talks)

Every story has a footnote citation to its primary source. If a day's specific story brief cannot be filled from real sources, surface the gap to the founder. Never fill with invented narrative.

## Constraints (project rules, non-negotiable)

- **Nicene Creed orthodoxy** baseline. Every devotional affirms Trinity, Incarnation, Atonement, bodily Resurrection.
- **Banned phrases** (zero tolerance): "in today's world", "let's unpack", "at the end of the day", "it's important to note", "in essence", "embark on a journey", "incredible journey", "God wants you to", "you'll be transformed", "join thousands of believers", "unlock your purpose", "so blessed to have you", "this begs the question", "you might be thinking..."
- **Multi-tradition charity** on contested points. Day 3 (indwelling vs. subsequent-empowering Spirit baptism) and Day 5 (substitutionary atonement vs. Christus Victor) — both faithful, neither resolved on the page.
- **Citations:** every patristic / contemporary quote in real academic form. Every story cited with a real primary source.
- **Brand terminology** in narrative copy: "daily readings", "a series", "the walk", "something to sit with". Never "devotional" / "spiritual journey" / "faith course" / "Bible study" in user-facing prose.
