## One-line theme

for us by name, carries us out of the barren place, and seats us at His table as sons.

## The spine

Mephibosheth is the whole thematic in one man. He is crippled by an accident that
happened while people were fleeing (2 Samuel 4:4) — hurt in someone else's disaster.
He lives in Lo-debar, across the Jordan, **sheltered in the house of Machir — a
wealthy magnate who later provisions David's army (2 Samuel 17:28-29)**. He is
hidden, not destitute. The popular "Lo-debar means no pasture / barren wasteland"
reading FAILS on both counts (see source pack §2) and must not be printed. By every ancient
convention he should be executed: he is the last male heir of the deposed dynasty.
Instead a king asks, "Is there anyone left?" — and sends for him. He is not summoned
to trial. He is carried to a table. And the last line of the chapter refuses to
tidy up: he ate at the king's table, **and he was lame in both feet**. The healing
the story gives is not the healing he would have asked for. That tension is the
series' honesty, and it is not resolved before Day 5.

## 7-day chiastic outline

### Day 1 (A) — Hook: Is There Anyone Left

- Anchor: 2 Samuel 9:1-4 — BSB
- Theme: A king with no obligation goes looking for someone with no claim. The
  search begins before the found man knows anything about it.
- Meta-story line: KINGDOM — David secure on the throne, and the first thing
  secure power does here is go looking for a survivor.

### Day 2 (B) — Build: What the Breach Costs

- Anchor: 2 Corinthians 5:18-21 — BSB
- Theme: Reconciliation is not God changing His mind about us. It is God absorbing
  the cost of the breach Himself. Why an offer of peace requires someone to pay.

### Day 3 (C) — Pivot: The God Who Comes Down Looking

- Anchor: Ezekiel 34:11-16 — BSB (with Luke 19:10)
- Theme: God refuses to delegate the search. "I Myself will search for My sheep."
- The "aha": Everywhere Scripture describes rescue, God moves DOWNWARD. Lo-debar
  is not a detour on the way to the palace — descending IS the method.

### Day 4 (B-prime) — Apply: Robe, Ring, Sandals

- Anchor: Luke 15:20-24 — BSB
- Theme: Rescue and rehabilitation are not the same thing. The father's gifts are
  all restorations of _function and standing_, not comforts. What healing looks
  like when it is slower than deliverance.

### Day 5 (A-prime) — Resolve: At the Table, Still Lame

- Anchor: Ephesians 2:4-7 — BSB (with Psalm 103:1-5, KJV)
- Theme: Seated with Christ, crowned with mercy — and still carrying the limp.
  Adoption confers standing before it confers repair. The crown is not a reward
  for walking well.

### Day 6 — Recap

- The five takeaways integrated; Further Your Learning with the verified study set.

### Day 7 — Sabbath

- Anchor: Zephaniah 3:17 — BSB
- One-line invitation: sit at the table without earning the chair.
