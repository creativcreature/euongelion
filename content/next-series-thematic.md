# Drawing Near

## The spine

Nearness was always the point, and it was never freely available — until it
was. The Law ran an honest, rationed system of approach and said its own
ceiling out loud. Christ tore the veil, and the veil was His flesh.

What that opens is not **permission**. It is **proximity** — you may come,
freely, in person, as a child and not a petitioner. And freedom like that is
held the way you hold anything precious and shared: by love.

Two frames, nested. Outer: **obedience → relationship**. Inner:
**permission → proximity**. Every day must point at one of them.

The weight of the week sits on the INVITATION, not the warning. This is an
encouragement: you may draw near, in the freedom of Christ, today. The
responsibility is how you carry that — and it is the last movement, not the
theme.

## The rhyme the week is built on

Genesis 3:24 — God sets cherubim and a flaming sword "to keep **the way** of
the tree of life."
Romans 14:13 — "that no man put a stumblingblock or an occasion to fall in
his brother's **way**."

Both are someone obstructing a road to life. God does it once and it is
mercy, because Genesis 3:22 states the reason and the reason is not rage:
"lest he put forth his hand, and take also of the tree of life, and eat, and
**live for ever**." He bars the tree so a broken man is not sealed broken
forever. Paul then says the one road you are still capable of blocking is
your brother's. Day 1 and Day 5 are that sentence pointed two directions.

Note Genesis 3:8 — they hid from the presence before they were driven out.
The distance was chosen before it was enforced.

## Origins and destiny — the loop the series closes

**Origins.** Genesis 1:26–28 binds two things in one sentence: made in our
**image** (צֶלֶם _tselem_) and "let them have **dominion**" (רָדָה _radah_).
Psalm 8:5 says it outright — "thou hast **crowned him with glory** and
honour." An image is what a king sets up in a province he is not standing
in, so the territory knows whose it is; Daniel 3:1 narrates exactly that
practice. Humanity was placed in creation as the proclamation of a King.

**Destiny.** 2 Corinthians 3:18 — "changed into the same **image** (εἰκών)
from **glory to glory**." The image made in Genesis 1, hidden in Genesis 3,
is restored by beholding glory unveiled. Romans 8 runs the same road inside
one chapter: adoption (8:15) → heirs and joint-heirs (8:17) → "conformed to
the **image** of his Son… them he also **glorified**" (8:29–30).

## What holiness is

קָדַשׁ _qadash_ — the lexicon's first gloss is **"be set apart."** קֹדֶשׁ
_qodesh_ is **"apartness."** Holiness is not _very good_; it is **otherness**.
God is not the best thing in the category — He is outside the category, and
1 Samuel 2:2 defines it that way: "There is none holy as the Lord: **for
there is none beside thee**."

**The three Holies.** Hebrew has no superlative form, so repetition carries
it. Doubling intensifies — Isaiah 26:3's "perfect peace" is _shalom shalom_,
and the KJV translators rendered the doubling as "perfect." Tripling is the
ceiling, and Isaiah 6:3 and Revelation 4:8 give it to one attribute only.
Emphasis and uniqueness at once.

**Glory is weight.** כָּבוֹד _kavod_ comes from כָּבַד _kavad_, "be heavy."
Not sparkle — heft, the mass of what is in the room. Paul makes the same pun
at 2 Corinthians 4:17, "an eternal **weight** of glory." Thread it through
Days 2, 4 and 7.

## The seven days (SA-029 shape: A B C B′ A′ + recap + sabbath)

**Day 1 — A — "Crowned, And Hiding."** Anchor: Genesis 1:26–28; Psalm 8:5;
Genesis 3:8, 22–24.
Origins first, so the exile can land. Made in His image, crowned with glory,
given dominion — the King's image set in His province. Then the image hid.
Then the exile, and the stated reason is the tree of life. The distance is
real, authored, and protective.

**Day 2 — B — "As Near As Blood Could Bring You."** Anchor: Leviticus 17:11;
Hebrews 10:4.
Why so far: holiness as apartness (1 Samuel 2:2), and Isaiah 6 — "Holy, holy,
holy… the whole earth is full of his glory," then "Woe is me! for I am
undone," then a live coal **from off the altar**, and the iniquity is taken
away. Then why blood: "the life of the flesh is in the blood: and **I have
given it to you** upon the altar." Life is נֶפֶשׁ _nephesh_, so blood is not
currency, it is life; and the verb is _given_ — God supplies the means of His
own approach. Hebrew of the day: קׇרְבָּן _qorbân_ (offering) from קָרַב
_qârab_ (to come near). The offering is the brought-near thing. Then the
ceiling: one man, one room, one day a year, and Leviticus 16:17 says no one
else could even be in the tent. Hebrews 10:3–4 — a remembrance again made of
sins every year, and it was never possible.

**Day 3 — C — "It Is Finished." (THE HINGE.)** Anchor: John 19:30;
Hebrews 10:20.
Priest and offering at once. "Through the veil, that is to say, **his
flesh**" — the torn veil and the torn body are one tearing, and Hebrews says
so. Mark 15:38, top to bottom. τετέλεσται from τελέω; τέλος — Christ is the
_end_ of the law for righteousness (Romans 10:4), end and goal in one root.
Matthew 5:17: not καταλύω (destroy) but πληρόω (fill full). The rationing
ends.
Hebrews 10 is this week in chapter form: v1 the law could not perfect "the
comers thereunto" (τοὺς προσερχομένους, the drawing-near ones) → v20 the veil,
that is his flesh → v22 "let us draw near" (προσερχώμεθα). Same verb, opened.

**Day 4 — B′ — "Boldness To Enter." (THE HEART OF THE WEEK.)** Anchor:
Hebrews 10:19–22; Romans 8:15–17.
Day 2 inverted at every point: one man → anyone; one day → any day; by proxy
→ in person; rationed → free. The word is παρρησία — glossed "freedom of
speech; plainness; openness; **freedom**." Hebrews' word for how you enter the
holiest is a freedom word.
The privilege: Moses asked "shew me thy glory" and was told "there shall no
man see me, and live" (Exodus 33:18–20). 2 Corinthians 3 answers it directly —
the veil "done away in Christ" (v14), "where the Spirit of the Lord is, there
is **liberty**" (v17), "we all, with **open face** beholding… the glory of the
Lord, are changed into the same image, from glory to glory" (v18).
The inheritance: Romans 8:15–17, not the spirit of bondage but adoption —
_Abba_ — children, then heirs, joint-heirs, glorified together. And 1 Peter
2:9, "a **royal priesthood**," quoting Exodus 19:6. Day 2 had one priest who
could die; you are one now. **The holiness did not reduce by one degree. Your
standing changed.** That is the privilege, and it only lands because Day 2
made the otherness real.

**Day 5 — A′ — "Only Use Not Liberty."** Anchor: Hebrews 10:23–25;
Galatians 5:13.
Mirror of Day 1: kept out for protection, now let in — so how is it carried.
Hebrews gives its own answer in sequence: draw near (v22) → hold fast (v23) →
**consider one another** to provoke unto love and to good works (v24), not
forsaking the assembling (v25). Nearness produces company; the responsibility
is the third consequence of nearness, not a separate warning.
Galatians 5:13 — "called unto liberty; only use not liberty for an occasion
to the flesh, but **by love serve one another**." What He wants from the
nearness is חֶסֶד _chesed_, not performance: Hosea 6:6, and Jesus quotes it
twice, both times at people judging someone else over a table (Matthew 9:13)
and a sabbath (Matthew 12:7) — "ye would not have condemned the guiltless."
The walking is caused, not summoned: Ezekiel 36:26–27. Reverence and boldness
together: Hebrews 12:28–29, a kingdom that cannot be moved, and a consuming
fire. Act from your own conviction before God: Romans 14:5, 22. The
stumblingblock (Romans 14:13) gets **one beat** — a guardrail, not the climax.
Close on Romans 14:17.

**Day 6 — recap.** Anchor: Ephesians 2:13.
"Ye who sometimes were **far off** are made **nigh** by the blood of Christ" —
the week in eleven words. Then the horizon: Revelation 21:3, the tabernacle of
God is with men; 21:22, "**I saw no temple therein**"; 21:23, the glory of God
did lighten it. Day 2's one rationed room becomes the whole city.

**Day 7 — sabbath.** Anchor: James 4:8; Revelation 22:4.
"Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you." Revelation 22:3, no more
curse — Genesis 3 undone. And the last line of the series: **"they shall see
his face"** (22:4), which answers Exodus 33:20 and completes the open face of
2 Corinthians 3:18. Nearness with nothing required of it. Nothing added.

## Leviticus discipline (founder ruling 2026-08-28)

Use Leviticus for its **vocabulary and its logic of approach** — _qorbân_ /
_qârab_, holiness as apartness, blood as life, the honest annual limit. Do
**not** tour the sacrificial taxonomy: no burnt/meal/peace/sin/trespass
survey, no Day of Atonement procedural walk-through. Leviticus 17:11 is the
verse the week needs, and it earns its place by explaining _why blood_, not by
cataloguing offerings.

## Baggage — name it plainly, once, early, then teach the text

1. **Day 1 — "the exile was punishment, full stop."** Genesis 3:22 gives the
   stated reason and it is the tree of life. Correct once, move on.
2. **Day 2 — "an angry Father extracting payment from a kind Son."**
   Leviticus 17:11 says God _gave_ the blood upon the altar; Hebrews 10:20
   says the veil was His own flesh. God is the giver, not the extorted party.
   State it once; do not argue with a caricature for a page.
3. **Day 5 — "freedom means it does not matter what I do."**
   1 Corinthians 10:23. Once.

## Verification flags — where this theme usually goes wrong

1. **Do NOT use Revelation 22:14.** KJV reads "they that do his
   commandments"; the critical text reads "wash their robes" (ποιοῦντες τὰς
   ἐντολάς vs. πλύνοντες τὰς στολάς — near-identical in uncials). A series
   arguing Christ ended the law must not close on a verse that appears to make
   commandment-keeping the entry condition. **Use Revelation 22:2 and 22:4.**
2. **Hosea 6:6 "not… but" is comparative Hebrew idiom, not abolition.** The
   internal control is Mark 12:33, "is **more than** all whole burnt
   offerings." Without this, Day 5 contradicts Day 2.
3. **Daniel 3 is Aramaic** — צְלֵם (H6755) is _cognate_ to Hebrew צֶלֶם
   (H6754), not the identical lexeme. The concept link is sound; do not claim
   "the same word."
4. **"No other attribute of God is tripled"** is a universal negative. Verify
   it or soften it to the throne-room visions specifically.
5. **Isaiah 26:3** — the _shalom shalom_ doubling is invisible in the KJV
   English. Show the Hebrew, and confirm the MT reading.
6. **τετέλεσται "stamped PAID IN FULL on a receipt"** — popular and
   overstated. τελέω does carry "to pay," so the idea is safe, but LEAD with
   τέλος / Romans 10:4, which is airtight. Use the receipt claim only if a
   source verifies it; otherwise cut it.
7. **Galatians 5:14** — "all the law **is fulfilled** in one word." If that
   verb is πληρόω-family it seams back to Matthew 5:17 and closes the loop.
   CONFIRM THE GREEK before building on it.
8. **The veil** — the text says "from the top to the bottom" (Mark 15:38).
   Let the detail carry it; do not narrate God's hands doing the tearing.
9. **The ANE royal-image background** (kings erecting steles in conquered
   provinces) is external scholarship. Source it, or lean on Daniel 3 and
   Genesis 1:26's own dominion clause, which are internal.
