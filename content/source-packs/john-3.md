## 2. OLD TESTAMENT ROOTS

### Genesis 1:1–3 (BSB) — _ruach_ (רוּחַ) and the first light

> **Gen 1:1** — _In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth._
> **Gen 1:2** — _Now the earth was formless and void, and darkness was over the surface of the deep. And the Spirit of God was hovering over the surface of the waters._
> **Gen 1:3** — _And God said, "Let there be light," and there was light._

The Hebrew _ruach_ (רוּחַ) appears in Gen 1:2 and threads through the whole Hebrew Bible — same word for "wind," "breath," "spirit." Same word Jesus reaches for in John 3:8.

### Numbers 21:6–9 (BSB) — the bronze serpent

> **Num 21:6** — _So the LORD sent venomous snakes among the people, and many of the Israelites were bitten and died._
> **Num 21:7** — _Then the people came to Moses and said, "We have sinned by speaking against the LORD and against you. Intercede with the LORD so He will take the snakes away from us." So Moses interceded for the people._
> **Num 21:8** — _Then the LORD said to Moses, "Make a fiery serpent and mount it on a pole. When anyone who is bitten looks at it, he will live."_
> **Num 21:9** — _So Moses made a bronze snake and mounted it on a pole. If anyone who was bitten looked at the bronze snake, he would live._

This is the typology Jesus Himself names in John 3:14. Day 5's anchor.

### Ezekiel 36:25–27 (BSB) — clean water, new spirit

> **Ezek 36:25** — _I will also sprinkle clean water on you, and you will be clean. I will cleanse you from all your impurities and all your idols._
> **Ezek 36:26** — _I will give you a new heart and put a new spirit within you; I will remove your heart of stone and give you a heart of flesh._
> **Ezek 36:27** — _And I will put My Spirit within you and cause you to walk in My statutes and to carefully observe My ordinances._

The Old Testament backbone for John 3:5's "water and Spirit." Ezekiel had already promised this 600 years earlier.

### Habakkuk 2:4 (KJV) — _emunah_ (אמונה) and faithing

> _"Behold, his soul which is lifted up is not upright in him: but the just shall live by his faith."_

The Hebrew word here is _emunah_ (אמונה) — closer to "faithfulness" than "belief." Paul quotes it in Romans 1:17 and Galatians 3:11; Hebrews 10:38 quotes it again. Day 4's anchor for _pisteuō_ (πιστεύω) as living, ongoing trust.

### Genesis 1:3–4 (BSB) — light separated from darkness

> **Gen 1:3** — _And God said, "Let there be light," and there was light._
> **Gen 1:4** — _And God saw that the light was good, and He separated the light from the darkness._

Day 7's anchor — John 3:18–21's light/darkness verdict echoes the very first separation.

### Job 24:13–17 (KJV) — those who shun the light (Day 1 OT root)

> _"They are of those that rebel against the light; they know not the ways thereof, nor abide in the paths thereof. The murderer rising with the light killeth the poor and needy, and in the night is as a thief. … For the morning is to them even as the shadow of death: if one know them, they are in the terrors of the shadow of death."_

Companions Nicodemus's "by night" with the OT category of those who hide their deeds from light.

### Isaiah 49:6 (BSB) — light to the nations

> _"It is not enough for You to be My Servant, to raise up the tribes of Jacob and restore the protected ones of Israel. I will also make You a light for the nations, to bring My salvation to the ends of the earth."_

Day 6 anchor — when John 3:16 says "God so loved the world (kosmos)," it is enacting Isaiah's promise.

## 3. NEW TESTAMENT ECHOES

### Romans 6:5 (BSB) — united in His death (Day 5 NT echo)

> _"For if we have been united with Him like this in His death, we will certainly also be united with Him in His resurrection."_

Paul develops the death-baptism implication of John 3:14–15.

### Titus 3:5 (BSB) — washing of regeneration (Day 3 NT echo)

> _"He saved us, not by the righteous deeds we had done, but according to His mercy, through the washing of new birth and renewal by the Holy Spirit."_

The Greek _paliggenesia_ (παλιγγενεσία, "regeneration / new birth") is the doctrinal term that comes out of John 3:5.

### 1 Peter 1:23 (BSB) — born of imperishable seed (Day 2 NT echo)

> _"For you have been born again, not of perishable seed, but of imperishable, through the living and enduring word of God."_

The same verb Peter uses — _anagennaō_ (ἀναγεννάω) — picks up John 3:3's _gennēthē anōthen_.

### Colossians 1:13 (BSB) — rescue from darkness (Day 7 NT echo)

> _"He has rescued us from the dominion of darkness and brought us into the kingdom of His beloved Son."_

Paul's compressed restatement of John 3:18–21.

### Ephesians 5:8 (BSB) — children of light (Day 7 NT echo)

> _"For you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Walk as children of light."_

Paul's way of stating John 3:21's "whoever practices the truth comes into the Light."

### Hebrews 11:1 (BSB) — faith as substance (Day 4 NT echo)

> _"Now faith is the assurance of what we hope for and the certainty of what we do not see."_

The classical New Testament definition of _pistis_. Pairs with John 3:9–13's "how can these things be?" — faith is the substance of what knowledge has not yet caught up to.

### Romans 5:8 (BSB) — while we were still sinners (Day 6 NT echo)

> _"But God proves His love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us."_

Paul's gloss on John 3:16's "God so loved the world."

### 2 Timothy 1:7–8 (BSB) — secret discipleship (Day 1 NT echo)

> **2 Tim 1:7** — _For God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and self-control._
> **2 Tim 1:8** — _So do not be ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, or of me, His prisoner. Instead, share in suffering for the gospel by the power of God._

Paul's word to Timothy — the antidote to Nicodemus's posture.

## 4. HEBREW & GREEK VOCAB DATA (transliteration ALWAYS paired)

### _nukti_ (νυκτί) — Greek "by night" (Day 1)

- **Strong's:** G3571 (form of νύξ, _nyx_)
- **Transliteration:** _nukti_ (dative singular)
- **Pronunciation:** NOOK-tee
- **Definition:** night; figuratively: spiritual darkness, ignorance, hidden activity
- **Usage in John:** John uses νύξ/νυκτί with theological weight throughout — Judas leaves to betray Jesus "and it was night" (13:30); Nicodemus comes "by night" (3:2). The word names more than the time of day.

### _gennēthē anōthen_ (γεννηθῇ ἄνωθεν) — Greek "born again / from above" (Day 2)

- **Strong's:** G1080 (γεννάω, _gennaō_ — beget, give birth) + G509 (ἄνωθεν, _anōthen_ — from above / again)
- **Transliteration:** _gennēthē anōthen_
- **Pronunciation:** ghen-NAY-thay AH-no-thehn
- **Definition:** _Anōthen_ carries both meanings simultaneously — "from above" (origin) AND "again" (repetition). Greek allows the double-entendre; English translations have to pick one. The point: Nicodemus hears "again" (and asks the absurd question about returning to the womb) while Jesus has been saying "from above" all along.
- **Usage in John:** _Anōthen_ appears 5 times in John (3:3, 3:7, 3:31, 19:11, 19:23). In 3:31 ("the One who comes from above") it CAN ONLY mean "from above." Jesus is being deliberate.

### _pneuma_ (πνεῦμα) — Greek "wind / breath / spirit" (Day 3)

- **Strong's:** G4151
- **Transliteration:** _pneuma_
- **Pronunciation:** NYOO-mah
- **Definition:** breath, wind, spirit. The single word covers all three; English has separated them. The word forces the three-into-one move Jesus makes in John 3:8 — _the wind blows where it wishes ... so it is with everyone born of the Spirit._

### _ruach_ (רוּחַ) — Hebrew "wind / breath / spirit" (Day 3)

- **Strong's:** H7307
- **Transliteration:** _ruach_
- **Pronunciation:** ROO-akh
- **Definition:** breath, wind, spirit — same triple meaning as Greek _pneuma_. The Septuagint translates _ruach_ with _pneuma_. Genesis 1:2's "Spirit of God hovering over the waters" is _ruach Elohim_.

### _pisteuō_ (πιστεύω) — Greek "trust / believe / commit-to" (Day 4)

- **Strong's:** G4100
- **Transliteration:** _pisteuō_
- **Pronunciation:** pis-TOO-oh
- **Definition:** to trust, to entrust, to commit oneself to. The verb form Jesus uses in 3:15, 3:16, 3:18 is **present-active** — meaning ongoing, continuous trusting. Not a single past event ("believed"). Continuing ("trusting").
- **Note:** _pisteuō_ is a verb. The English noun "belief" loses the active-continuous shape. The "faithing" word the founder reaches for is closer to the Greek.

### _emunah_ (אמונה) — Hebrew "faithfulness / steadfastness" (Day 4)

- **Strong's:** H530
- **Transliteration:** _emunah_
- **Pronunciation:** eh-moo-NAH
- **Definition:** firmness, faithfulness, steadiness. From the verb _aman_ (אמן) — "to confirm, to support, to be faithful" (also the root of "amen"). Habakkuk 2:4: _"the just shall live by his emunah."_ This is what _pisteuō_ translates.

### _hupsōthēnai_ (ὑψωθῆναι) — Greek "to be lifted up" (Day 5)

- **Strong's:** G5312 (ὑψόω, _hupsoō_)
- **Transliteration:** _hupsōthēnai_ (aorist passive infinitive)
- **Pronunciation:** hoop-soh-THAY-nai
- **Definition:** to lift up, to exalt. The double meaning is the point: in John, Jesus' "being lifted up" refers BOTH to the cross (literal lifting) AND to the resurrection-ascension (exaltation). One verb carries both. John uses it of Jesus in 3:14, 8:28, and 12:32–34.

### _sōzō_ (σῴζω) — Greek "to save / rescue / preserve" (Day 6)

- **Strong's:** G4982
- **Transliteration:** _sōzō_
- **Pronunciation:** SOH-zoh
- **Definition:** to save, to rescue, to deliver, to heal, to preserve. Wider than English "save." Used of physical rescue from drowning (Matt 14:30), of healing from disease (Mark 5:34), and of salvation from sin (John 3:17). The same word: rescue.

### _kosmos_ (κόσμος) — Greek "world / order / arrangement" (Day 6)

- **Strong's:** G2889
- **Transliteration:** _kosmos_
- **Pronunciation:** KOS-mos
- **Definition:** ordered system, arrangement, world. John uses _kosmos_ 78 times in his gospel — more than any other NT book. The "world" God so loved (3:16) is not just earth-as-planet; it is the whole humanly-disordered system, the entire arrangement that has gone wrong.

### _phōs_ (φῶς) — Greek "light" (Day 7)

- **Strong's:** G5457
- **Transliteration:** _phōs_
- **Pronunciation:** fohs
- **Definition:** light. John 1:4–5: _"In Him was life, and that life was the light of men. The Light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it."_ The word frames the prologue (1:4–9) and the verdict (3:19–21). Bookends.

## 5. EXTERNAL QUOTES — verified from public-domain or already-cited sources

### Augustine on the night-coming Nicodemus (Day 1)

> _"He came to Jesus by night, that he might come to the Light. He sought Christ, but in the dark; for as yet he understood not Christ to be the Light. Yet he came: he came as a man comes to a window in the dark, that he may see if any light shine within."_

_Citation:_ Augustine of Hippo, **Tractates on the Gospel of John**, Tractate 11, on John 2:23–3:5 (~415 AD). Public domain. (Note: this English rendering is the Schaff translation, NPNF Series I, Vol. VII.)

### Augustine on the heart's restlessness (Day 2 candidate / Day 7 candidate)

> _"You have made us for yourself, O Lord, and our hearts are restless until they rest in you."_

_Citation:_ Augustine of Hippo, **Confessions**, Book I.1, trans. Henry Chadwick (Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1991). Verified per `content/reference/SOURCE-BANK.md`.

### Athanasius on the incarnation (Day 5 — bronze serpent → Christ)

> _"He has been manifested in a human body for this reason only, out of the love and goodness of His Father, for the salvation of us men."_

_Citation:_ Athanasius of Alexandria, **On the Incarnation**, §1 (~318 AD). Public domain.

### Athanasius on theosis (Day 2 candidate — born from above)

> _"For He was made man that we might be made God."_

_Citation:_ Athanasius, **On the Incarnation**, §54 (~318 AD). Note: the second "God" is theosis-language — sharing in God's life as creatures restored, not ontological equality. The writer must define this in line if the quote is used.

### John Calvin on John 3:3 (Day 2)

> _"To be born again means to begin to be a new creature, and to be reformed by the Spirit of God, who renews us in the image of Him that created us."_

_Citation:_ John Calvin, **Commentary on John**, vol. 1, on John 3:3 (1553), trans. William Pringle, Calvin Translation Society (Edinburgh, 1847). Public domain.

### Calvin on John 3:8 (Day 3)

> _"Christ employs an apt comparison, when he compares the Spirit to the wind; for as the power of the wind is manifest, while its origin is concealed, so the work of the Spirit is visible in his effects, while the manner in which he acts is not perceived."_

_Citation:_ John Calvin, **Commentary on John**, on John 3:8 (1553). Public domain.

### Matthew Henry on the bronze serpent (Day 5)

> _"Many of the Israelites were stung when bitten by the fiery serpents, and many were healed when looking up at the brazen serpent. Christ is exalted to give repentance and remission. Look unto him and be saved, all ye ends of the earth."_

_Citation:_ Matthew Henry, **Commentary on the Whole Bible**, on Numbers 21:4–9 (1708–1710). Public domain.

### Calvin on John 3:16 (Day 6)

> _"By these words Christ intended chiefly to draw the eyes of believers to himself alone, that they may not seek life anywhere else than in him. … God did not spare his only-begotten Son, that he might rescue our souls from death."_

_Citation:_ John Calvin, **Commentary on John**, on John 3:16 (1553). Public domain.

### Tim Keller on John 3:16 (Day 6 — modern echo, optional)

> _"To be loved but not known is comforting but superficial. To be known and not loved is our greatest fear. But to be fully known and truly loved is, well, a lot like being loved by God."_

_Citation:_ Tim Keller, **The Meaning of Marriage** (New York: Dutton, 2011), p. 95. Verified per SOURCE-BANK.

### N.T. Wright on the kingdom (Day 1 — modern echo, optional)

> _"The gospel is not about going to heaven when you die. It is about God's kingdom coming on earth as it is in heaven."_

_Citation:_ N.T. Wright, **Simply Jesus** (New York: HarperOne, 2011), p. 217. Verified per SOURCE-BANK.

## 6. STORY CANDIDATES (real, named, citable — never invented)

The writer agent must use one per day for the daily Story section. Each candidate listed here has a real primary source. Pick the strongest fit for the day's theme; expand the pack if a better story is needed.

### Day 1 — secret faith beginning in private

- **Joseph of Arimathea & Nicodemus at the burial (John 19:38–42)** — the same Nicodemus, ~3 years later, openly bringing 75 pounds of myrrh and aloes to anoint Jesus' body. Internal NT cross-reference. Strongest single match — the secret seeker becomes the open mourner.
- **C.S. Lewis's slow conversion (1929–1931)** — documented in **Surprised by Joy** (London: Bles, 1955). Lewis describes himself as "the most reluctant convert in all England" — converted alone in his bedroom, then slowly, then publicly. Citation: Lewis, _Surprised by Joy_, p. 228–229.
- **The North Korean underground church** — documented testimonies via **Open Doors** annual World Watch List reports (2024 and prior). Citable but use with care — most names are protected for safety.

### Day 2 — regeneration / born from above

- **John Newton's transformation** — slave-trader turned pastor; his **Authentic Narrative** (1764) documents the moment of his conversion in a storm (March 21, 1748). Wrote "Amazing Grace." Primary source: Newton, _An Authentic Narrative of Some Remarkable and Interesting Particulars_. Verified through extensive scholarship.
- **Charles Colson's prison conversion (1973–1974)** — documented in **Born Again** (1976). The Watergate-era political operator; conversion verified by his subsequent founding of Prison Fellowship (1976). Citation: Colson, _Born Again_ (Old Tappan, NJ: Chosen Books, 1976).

### Day 3 — wind moving where it wills

- **Augustine's mother Monica praying him into faith over 17 years** — documented in **Confessions** Books III–IX. Primary source: Augustine, _Confessions_ (~400 AD), trans. Chadwick. Day 3 fits because Monica's persistence was a _ruach_ the son could neither see nor predict.
- **The Iranian house church movement (post-1979)** — extensively documented; cite the **Operation World** 2024 country profile for Iran or a specific peer-reviewed study (e.g., Mark Bradley, **Iran and Christianity**, Continuum, 2008).

### Day 4 — stepping before knowing

- **George Müller's Bristol orphanages (1836–1898)** — Müller refused to ask anyone for money; recorded every prayer and answer in his journals. Day 4 fits because Müller was always stepping before knowing how the next meal would arrive. Primary source: **Autobiography of George Müller** (Westwood, NJ: Barbour, 1984 reprint of 1872 original) or any of his published journals.
- **Helen Keller's "moment at the well" (April 5, 1887)** — documented in **The Story of My Life** (1903). Day 4 fits if framed as the prelinguistic leap into a meaning-bearing world. Citation: Keller, _The Story of My Life_ (New York: Doubleday, 1903), Ch. 4.

### Day 5 — looking up to be healed

- **The 33 Chilean miners (Aug 5–Oct 13, 2010)** — buried 700 m below Chile's San José Mine for 69 days; rescued via a capsule called _Fénix_. Their shift foreman Luis Urzúa was the last man up. Day 5 fits if framed as "looking up at the rescue suspended above them." Primary source: **Operación San Lorenzo** (Chilean government report, 2010); journalist Jonathan Franklin, **33 Men: Inside the Miraculous Survival and Dramatic Rescue of the Chilean Miners** (Putnam, 2011).
- **Corrie ten Boom in Ravensbrück (1944)** — looking up in a place designed to keep faces down. Primary source: ten Boom, **The Hiding Place** (Grand Rapids: Chosen Books, 1971). Verified per SOURCE-BANK.

### Day 6 — God so loved (rescue, kosmos)

- **The Chilean miners rescue** (move from Day 5 if used there; otherwise cite here) — a global audience watching, every man pulled up by name.
- **The Apollo 13 reentry (April 17, 1970)** — the world watching, the crew pulled home through a fire of their own making. Primary source: **Lost Moon** (Lovell & Kluger, 1994; reissued as _Apollo 13_).
- **Dietrich Bonhoeffer choosing to return to Germany from New York in 1939** — knowing he would likely die. Primary source: Eberhard Bethge, **Dietrich Bonhoeffer: A Biography**, rev. ed. (Augsburg Fortress, 2000), Chapter 13. The point: love that walks toward danger is the shape of John 3:16.

### Day 7 — light breaking after darkness

- **Anne Frank's diary entry, July 15, 1944** — _"In spite of everything, I still believe that people are really good at heart."_ Citation: **The Diary of a Young Girl**, definitive ed. (Doubleday, 1995), p. 332. Day 7 fits if framed carefully — Frank did not survive; the light was theological, not biographical.
- **The Christmas Truce, 1914 (No-Man's-Land, Western Front)** — soldiers from opposite trenches sang carols, exchanged gifts, played football. Primary source: Stanley Weintraub, **Silent Night: The Story of the World War I Christmas Truce** (Free Press, 2001).
- **Augustine's morning in the Milan garden (August 386)** — _"Tolle, lege"_ ("take up and read"). Primary source: Augustine, **Confessions**, Book VIII.12.

## 7. NOTES FOR THE WRITER AGENT

1. **One spine, seven slices.** Every daily anchors on John 3:1–21. The daily verses overlap deliberately (Day 4 and Day 5 both touch 3:13–14). The deep dive is the only place that walks the whole passage in one sitting.
2. **Translation discipline.** BSB primary; KJV for poetic register (Habakkuk, John 3:1 KJV alternative, etc.); ASV when paired with Greek vocab. Validator rejects NIV/ESV/NASB/NRSV.
3. **Transliteration discipline.** Every Hebrew/Greek occurrence MUST have transliteration nearby. The validator audits this. Pattern: _gennēthē anōthen_ (γεννηθῇ ἄνωθεν), _ruach_ (רוּחַ).
4. **Sleep-pathway voice.** Define every theological term in line at first appearance. _Kerygma_, _theosis_, _anōthen_, _pneuma_, _ruach_, _kosmos_, _sōzō_, _paliggenesia_, _hupsōthēnai_ — all need a one-line gloss when introduced. The reader is intelligent but biblically untrained.
5. **Story discipline.** Real, named, citable. Footnote every story to its primary source. Never invent.
6. **Imagery discipline.** Every image briefed contextually. Reference manifest at `docs/image-library-manifest.json`. If the manifest's candidate is weak, surface the gap honestly.
7. **Video discipline.** Each video brief names the specific theological move it must teach. Default vetted creators: BibleProject, Don Carson. Confirm with founder if extending.
8. **The pivot is Day 4 (3:9–13 — "How can these things be?").** Write the deep dive's Day-4 corresponding section first to anchor the chiastic structure. Then expand outward.
9. **Day 6 contains John 3:16.** The most-read verse in Christianity. Write it last (or near last) so the rest of the dailies have earned the way to it.
10. **Day 7 closes the chiasm** by returning to light/darkness — the same image that opened Day 1's "by night."
