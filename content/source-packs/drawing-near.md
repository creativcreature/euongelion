**Heb 10:1 → 10:22 is the same lexeme, G4334.** A participle in v1 ("the comers
thereunto" — the drawing-near ones, whom the law could not perfect) and a
subjunctive in v22 ("let us draw near"). That is the week in one word.

**Galatians 5:14 carries a textual variant** (the tag is `N(k)O`; the Byzantine/TR
reading differs from NA28). The _lexeme_ is πληρόω on either reading, so the seam
back to Matthew 5:17 holds. Do not build on the tense.

**τετέλεσται** — lead with τέλος / Romans 10:4, which is airtight. The "stamped PAID
IN FULL on a receipt" claim is popular and overstated; τελέω does carry a paying
sense (τέλος is glossed _goal/tax_), but no source in this pack verifies the receipt
image. **Do not use the receipt claim.**

## Verification flags — resolved

1. **Revelation 22:14 — DO NOT USE.** Confirmed from the corpus: this repo's KJV
   reads "Blessed are they that **do his commandments**, that they may have right to
   the tree of life." A week arguing Christ is the τέλος of the law must not close on
   a verse that reads as commandment-keeping being the entry condition. **Close on
   Revelation 22:2 and 22:4 instead.**
2. **Hosea 6:6 is comparative idiom, not abolition.** Internal control confirmed
   verbatim: Mark 12:33 — "is **more than** all whole burnt offerings and
   sacrifices." Always pair them; without it Day 5 contradicts Day 2.
3. **Daniel 3 is Aramaic.** צְלֵם (H6755) is _cognate_ to Hebrew צֶלֶם (H6754), not
   the identical lexeme. State the concept link; never say "the same word."
4. **"No other attribute of God is tripled"** — a universal negative this pack does
   not verify. **Soften to the throne-room visions specifically** (Isaiah 6:3,
   Revelation 4:8).
5. **Isaiah 26:3 _shalom shalom_** — the doubling is invisible in KJV English and no
   Hebrew text in this pack confirms the MT reading. **Cut it**, or state it only as
   a general feature of Hebrew emphasis without hanging Isaiah 26:3 on it.
6. **The veil** — the text says "from the top to the bottom" (Mark 15:38). Let the
   detail carry it. Do not narrate God's hands doing the tearing.
7. **ANE royal-image background** — external scholarship, unsourced in this pack.
   Lean on Daniel 3:1 (internal, as a cognate practice) and Genesis 1:26's own
   dominion clause.
