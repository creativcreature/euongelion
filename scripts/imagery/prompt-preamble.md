The PREAMBLE body was founder-approved 2026-08-15. The **metadata and crop clauses
below were replaced in rev B** — the square-master pipeline they described was the
mathematical cause of round 1's failure and is retired. Do not restore it.

Assembled as: `PREAMBLE` + `\n\nSUBJECT: ` + subject + `\n\n` + the crop clause
**matching the master being produced** (landscape or portrait — they are different).

## PREAMBLE

FULL BLEED — CRITICAL: the artwork fills the entire frame edge to edge, corner to corner. NO paper margin, NO cream or white border, NO print edge, NO frame, NO vignette.

NO BARE PAPER — CRITICAL: every region of the frame is PRINTED. There is NO blank cream void, NO unprinted background, NO subject floating on bare paper. Even the quietest area carries something — a dot gradient, a wash of tone, paper texture holding ink.

PRINTED IS NOT POPULATED — EQUALLY CRITICAL: printing a region does NOT mean filling it with scenery. A frame may be mostly quiet warm cream carrying nothing but a faint tonal gradient, and that is CORRECT when the composition calls for it. Do NOT add sky detail, cloud banks, hills, terraces, distant towns or other landscape furniture to "complete" a frame. Add nothing that the stated subject does not require. Quiet is the goal in a quiet composition; blankness is the only thing forbidden.

GROUNDED, NEVER FLOATING — CRITICAL: every depicted object must occupy a physically coherent environment with believable contact, attachment, gravity and anatomy. A quiet frame still has a continuous printed ground, wall, water, earth or atmosphere. Never isolate a specimen as a floating emblem in cream space. Surrealism may alter scale, repetition, viewpoint or juxtaposition, but it must not invent malformed anatomy, impossible joints or fake object construction.

SCALE MUST BE LEGIBLE — CRITICAL: where the concept depends on something being enormous or tiny, the frame MUST contain a readable scale reference so the size relationship is visible — a small robed figure, a doorway, a distant treeline, a horizon. "Enormous" is meaningless in an empty field: an object alone on a plain reads as an ordinary object photographed close, not as a giant one. A single small figure counts as the subject, not as forbidden background filler — the instruction against background figures forbids CROWDS and bystanders, never the one figure that makes the idea readable.

COVERAGE DISCIPLINE — CRITICAL: the ink percentage stated in the subject is a HARD BUDGET, not a mood. If the subject says 20–35% ink, then 65–80% of the frame MUST remain quiet warm cream carrying only faint tone. Render the stated subject and then STOP — do not fill the remaining space with anything. Resist the urge to complete, balance or enrich the frame. An unfilled frame that holds its budget is correct; a beautiful frame that overshoots the budget is a failure and will be rejected.

Risograph screen-print illustration in strict two-color duotone: deep cobalt ultramarine blue ink on warm cream paper. Heavy visible Ben-Day halftone dots forming every tone, paper grain, slight misregistration leaving a faint crimson offset edge on some contours. No gray tones — every shadow is blue dot density. Flat printed poster aesthetic, no photorealism. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers or words.

HANDS AND ANATOMY — CRITICAL: hands are the single most common failure and must be correct. Exactly five fingers per hand, believable knuckles and joints, natural weight and grip, thumbs opposed correctly, wrists attached at a plausible angle. Do NOT render splayed fan-like fingers, extra or fused digits, boneless tapering fingers, or a hand whose palm and back are both visible. Prefer hands that are DOING something physical — gripping, cupping, pouring, pressing into clay — because a working hand is anatomically anchored. Where a hand is not the subject, keep it partly obscured by cloth, angle, shadow or the frame edge rather than fully displayed. Feet, shoulders and necks follow the same rule: correct count, correct joints, correct load-bearing.

NOT AI ARTWORK — CRITICAL: this must read as a real printed illustration made by a person, not as generated digital art. FORBIDDEN TELLS: airbrushed gradient haze and soft glow bloom; every area rendered at the same obsessive level of detail; bilateral symmetry in natural forms; ornamental filigree or scrollwork added as decoration; uniformly smooth contours; a plastic or chrome sheen; perfectly even lighting with no committed light direction; "epic" cinematic vignetting. INSTEAD: uneven ink laydown with visible roller texture, some passages left deliberately flat and undescribed while one area carries the detail, asymmetric and slightly irregular hand-cut shapes, honest registration slip, a single decisive light direction, and the restraint of a printer working two colours with a limited screen.

PEOPLE — CRITICAL: figures are REGION- AND PERIOD-SPECIFIC, never a generic Western default. This is an ancient Levantine / Judean setting: Middle Eastern Semitic people, dark hair, dark brows, dark eyes, some with dark beards. Period dress ONLY — rough woven tunic, draped mantle, head cloth or veil, sandals or bare feet. NO modern clothing.
SKIN TONE IS CRITICAL AND MUST BE VISIBLE IN THE PRINT: skin is brown to deep brown and is therefore rendered in DENSE blue halftone, clearly and obviously DARKER in value than the cream paper ground. Skin must NEVER be left as bare cream paper and must never read as pale or light — in this duotone, unprinted paper means white skin, which is WRONG.
ABSOLUTELY NOT: European, Anglo, Nordic or generic AI white faces, pale skin, light or blonde hair, modern haircuts.
Faces stay anonymous through POSE — seen from behind, in profile, head bowed, or shadowed by a head covering. NEVER a blacked-out featureless void where a face should be. A visible face is fine when it is genuinely region-accurate.

## CROP_CLAUSE_LANDSCAPE

Use with the 2400×1600 (3:2) master.

COMPOSITION FOR CROPPING — this is a WIDE 3:2 landscape frame, compose it as one. The whole conceptual device — the subject and the golden light together — must survive a centre crop to a SQUARE and to a 1.83:1 letterbox. In practice: keep everything essential inside the central 66% of the width and the central 75% of the height. If the idea has a strong vertical axis, compress that axis so its top and bottom both fall inside that band — a shape whose ends leave the band is a failed composition. You are NOT required to centre the subject: place it off-centre within the safe band and let the quiet fall to one side. The outer margin stays printed and quiet — toned, never blank, never filled with added scenery.

## CROP_CLAUSE_PORTRAIT

Use with the 1800×2400 (3:4) master.

COMPOSITION FOR CROPPING — this is a TALL 3:4 portrait frame, compose it as one. The whole conceptual device must survive a centre crop to a narrow 1:2 and to a 3:5. In practice: keep everything essential inside the central 66% of the width, using the full height freely. This frame is where a vertical idea belongs — let it run tall. The outer margin stays printed and quiet — toned, never blank, never filled with added scenery.

## Why each clause exists (do not drop these)

| Clause                             | Defect it fixes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| FULL BLEED                         | 17 of 21 masters came back with a cream paper border. `sharp.trim()` could not remove it — the gradient is too soft.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| NO BARE PAPER                      | Subjects floated on blank cream. Founder: "no stark nothing backgrounds." An earlier "leave quiet discardable margin" clause caused this — it read as permission to leave the frame empty.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| PRINTED IS NOT POPULATED           | **Added 2026-08-15 after a second failure.** The original fix over-corrected: it demanded sky detail, ground texture and distant landscape in every frame, which produced 33 plates at a mean 78% ink coverage that the founder read as "all depicting the same devotional." Round 2's first proof round then missed its coverage targets on 3 of 5 plates because this clause outranked the plate-specific direction. Quiet and blank are different things; only blank was ever forbidden.                         |
| PEOPLE / region + period           | Unspecified period defaults to contemporary Western. Produced a boy in a sweatshirt and jeans.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| SKIN AS PRINTED VALUE              | **The critical one.** In cobalt-on-cream duotone the cream paper is the only other ink, so unprinted skin _is_ white skin. Naming an ethnicity without forcing dot density still outputs a white person.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Faces by pose                      | Founder dislikes blacked-out faces; anonymity must come from posture, angle and head covering. A visible region-accurate face is acceptable.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| CROP_CLAUSE_LANDSCAPE / \_PORTRAIT | Saliency cropping destroyed meaning (it kept a hand and deleted the kneeling figure), so composition must carry the crop instead. **Rev B replaced the single square master.** Deriving all eight site ratios from one square left a crop-safe zone of only the central 60%×27% — 16% of the frame — which forced the centred, padded compositions the founder rejected. Two masters now serve their own families: landscape 3:2 (safe zone ≈50%) and portrait 3:4 (≈60%). Social ratios are out of scope entirely. |

## Verification gate — run before install

1. **Border** — sample 3 pixels down each edge of the 1408×768 centre crop; all must be ink, not paper.
2. **Blank paper** — bare-paper coverage across the whole frame must be ~0%.
3. **Figures** — open the image and confirm period dress, dense-dot skin, no blacked-out faces.

A build passing 1 and 2 can still fail 3. Step 3 is a human look, not a measurement.
