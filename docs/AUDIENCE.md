# Target Audience

**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** January 16, 2026

---

## The Core Truth

We're not building for "users." We're building for **lost sheep finding their way home**.

These are real people in real situations. The pathways (Sleep, Awake, Shepherd) are internal tools — externally, we meet people as people, not categories.

---

## Who We're Building For

### The Drifted

**Who they are:**

- Once had faith, now distant
- Didn't leave dramatically — just slowly stopped showing up
- Too busy, too tired, too distracted to notice they drifted
- Feel guilty about it but don't know how to come back
- Ages 25-45, often professionals, parents, or both

**What they feel:**

- "I used to pray, but I don't even know how anymore"
- "I believe in God, I just don't... do anything about it"
- "Church feels irrelevant to my actual life"
- "I'm too far gone / too busy / too tired"

**What they need:**

- A low-pressure re-entry point
- Content that speaks to real life, not church culture
- Permission to start small
- No judgment for where they've been

---

### The Skeptical Seeker

**Who they are:**

- Spiritually curious but wary of religion
- May have been hurt by church or Christians
- Deconstructing or post-evangelical
- Intellectually engaged, allergic to simplistic answers
- Often younger (20s-30s), educated, urban

**What they feel:**

- "I'm spiritual but not religious"
- "The church is hypocritical / irrelevant / harmful"
- "I have questions no one will answer honestly"
- "I want something real, not performance"

**What they need:**

- Honesty about hard questions
- Intellectual respect — don't dumb it down
- No manipulation, no guilt trips
- Space to explore without commitment pressure

---

### The Stuck Believer

**Who they are:**

- Committed Christians going through the motions
- Faith feels dry, routine, obligatory
- Know they should feel more but don't
- May be burned out from church service
- All ages, often long-time church attenders

**What they feel:**

- "I believe, but I don't feel anything"
- "My quiet time is just checking a box"
- "I'm spiritually stuck and don't know why"
- "There has to be more than this"

**What they need:**

- Fresh perspective on familiar truths
- Depth they haven't encountered
- Challenge without condemnation
- Rekindled wonder

---

### The Weary Shepherd

**Who they are:**

- Pastors, ministry leaders, small group leaders
- Those who feed others but aren't fed themselves
- Isolated in their struggles (can't be vulnerable with their flock)
- Exhausted from giving constantly
- Often middle-aged, in ministry 5+ years

**What they feel:**

- "I pour out but no one pours into me"
- "I'm supposed to have it together"
- "I'm lonely in leadership"
- "I need someone to shepherd me"

**What they need:**

- Content that feeds the feeder
- Permission to be struggling
- Deeper theological engagement
- Tools to extend their own shepherding

## Audience Characteristics (Across All)

### What Unites Them

| Trait                      | Description                                                   |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hungry**                 | Something is missing; they feel it even if they can't name it |
| **Busy**                   | Time-poor; need content that respects their schedule          |
| **Skeptical of Hype**      | Tired of slick Christian marketing; want authenticity         |
| **Mobile-First**           | Primarily accessing via phone; stolen moments in the day      |
| **Privacy-Conscious**      | Don't want to create accounts, share data, be tracked         |
| **Word-of-Mouth Oriented** | Trust peer recommendations over advertising                   |

### What They DON'T Want

- Judgment for where they are
- Pressure to commit before they're ready
- Churchy language that excludes
- Manipulation or guilt-based motivation
- Oversimplified answers to complex questions
- Another app demanding daily engagement streaks

### What They DO Want

- To be met where they are
- Honesty about hard things
- Beauty that feels intentional, not corporate
- Content they can trust
- Space to encounter God on their terms
- Something worth sharing with a friend who's struggling

## Language Implications

### Words We Use

- Journey (not program)
- Explore (not complete)
- Where you are (not your level)
- Walk with (not convert)
- Honest (not safe)

### Words We Avoid

- Lost (as a label for them)
- Sinner (in introductory contexts)
- Backslider
- Seeker-sensitive (internal jargon)
- Unchurched (clinical)
- Target audience (in their presence)

### Tone

- Warm but not saccharine
- Honest but not harsh
- Intelligent but not academic
- Inviting but not desperate
- Confident but not arrogant

## Social Media Audience & Messaging (Marketing Layer)

> Added for the Euangelion social pipeline. The four personas above are the full map; on social we play primarily to two of them at once, in public, with the scroll as our competition.

### The Dual-Purpose Mandate

Every post must work for **two readers at the same time**:

1. **The Outsider** — unfamiliar with, rejecting, or in denial of Jesus. Carries misconceptions (Christianity = rules, hypocrisy, anti-intellectual, judgment). May have been hurt by church. Maps to _The Skeptical Seeker_.
2. **The Insider Adrift** — already calls themselves Christian but is confused, apathetic, going through the motions, or genuinely seeking more. Maps to _The Drifted_ + _The Stuck Believer_.

The devotional content is always the **backbone** — the post is the doorway, the devotional is the room. We never resolve the whole thing in the caption; we open a wound or a question and point to the reading.

### The Messaging Engine (how a caption holds both readers)

1. **Name the misconception out loud.** Say the thing the outsider already believes about Christianity. They feel seen, not preached at.
2. **Turn the same mirror on the churchgoer.** Refuse to let the insider feel superior — "Most Christians live like they agree." No one is off the hook.
3. **State the real thing, plainly.** One true, concrete line. No jargon, no hedging.
4. **Leave them in it.** End on the tension or a question. Don't answer it. The answer is the devotional + their own conscience.

### Voice on Social (calibrated from the Public-Facing Language guide)

Same DNA as the app voice, dialed up for a hostile feed. The aim is to **shake people awake in a public forum** — disruptive and loud, but only ever because it's true.

- **Disruptive because it's true, never for engagement.** We can be loud and confrontational — when the loudness is conviction and the literal words of Scripture, not a manufactured hook. No engagement bait. No "argue with me" gimmicks. Purely genuine.
- **Earn the comment honestly.** People should _want_ to respond because the post named something real, not because we dared them to. Genuine reactions over manufactured ones, every time.
- **Saves and shares first (cold-start).** During the launch window, optimize for saves/shares — the signals Instagram uses to classify devotional content — and let comments come on their own.
- **Confrontational, not cruel.** Provoke self-examination, never contempt. Challenge without condemnation.
- **Plain and concrete.** Short sentences. Real words. Let questions breathe — and leave them unanswered when the answer is the reading or the reader's own conscience.
- **Intellectually honest.** Respect the skeptic's intelligence; never dumb it down or guilt-trip.

**The test for any bold line:** Would we say this because we believe it and Scripture says it — even if no one ever commented? If yes, ship it. If it only exists to bait a reply, cut it.

### Banned on Social (extends AI-CONTENT-CONSTRAINTS.md)

- The "it's not X, it's Y" formula
- Rhetorical question immediately answered
- Hype, exclamation marks, "journey," streak-bait
- Em-dash pile-ups and other AI tells
- Cheap clickbait that the devotional can't honor — the post must cash the check it writes

### Hashtag Strategy (dual-reach)

Mix three buckets, 8–12 total: **broad** (#faith #christianity #dailyreading), **niche/wedge** (#biblicalgreek #biblicalhebrew #wordstudy — the original-language differentiator), **branded** (#euangelion #goodnews). Include reach into the outsider's world where honest (#deconstruction #spiritualnotreligious) without baiting.

### Success on Social (by reader)

| Reader             | What a win looks like                                                                                                     |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The Outsider       | Stops scrolling. Comments to argue or confess. Sends it to a believing friend to ask "is this what you actually believe?" |
| The Insider Adrift | Feels caught, then drawn. Saves it. Taps through to the reading. Shares to their group.                                   |

North-star stays the same: **continued seeking, sharing with peers, and a tap into the devotional.**
