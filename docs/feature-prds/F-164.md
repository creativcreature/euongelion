# F-164: The sidebar is the player

## Problem

Founder, after seeing three mockups and choosing option A: "desktop sidebar
should contain universal player", having observed that the sidebar and the
reader's own panel were "basically redundant" — two sets of play controls
driving one element, showing the same thing.

Then, clarifying: "The sidebar (universal player) should also que other
devotionals, not just the chapters." Pressing play registered only the reading
you started, so Up Next was permanently empty — a queue of one.

## Solution

**The sidebar carries the whole player.** Added: a drag-to-position control with
elapsed and remaining, a previous-in-queue step, and speed, sleep timer and
chapters — reusing `SpeedSheet`, `SleepTimer` and `NarrationChapters` rather
than rebuilding them.

**Playing a reading queues its series behind it.** `queueForReading` finds the
series a reading belongs to and queues it FROM that day, so day three does not
offer to replay days one and two. Falls back to the reading alone when it
belongs to no series — a generated day is still a valid thing to be playing.

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- The sidebar has every control the reader's panel has.
- Up Next carries other devotionals, not only the current reading.

### Mobile

- Same sheet, same controls; every target stays 44px.
