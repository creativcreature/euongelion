## Problem

The founder asked for an audio-forward site with playlists, and asked that the
design be grounded in research rather than reflex: "use last 30 days skill and
mobbin for research and adhere to it."

Three things were wrong or unknown before this work:

1. The scroll mini bar shipped a forward-15 control that was desktop-only, so a
   phone showed a lone back-15 button. That reads as an omission, not a choice.
2. No one had checked our transport against what audio products actually ship.
3. The audio-forward strategy drafted alongside this work opened on a measured
   claim that 380 of 550 tracks return 404 in production, and made an R2
   migration the gate in front of every other piece of audio work.

## Solution

**Research, two independent passes.** Mobbin supplied 16 iOS screens and 4
recorded flows (ElevenReader, Spotify, Blinkist, Headway, The Atlantic, Waking
Up, Bloom, Finimize, Calm, Substack, Apple News, Apple Podcasts, NYTimes Audio,
Neuecast). `/last30days` supplied 61 items across 6 sources for the window
2026-07-20 to 2026-08-19. Both are written up in
`docs/audio/AUDIO-UX-PATTERN-RESEARCH-2026-08-19.md`, whose section 5 is binding
on `docs/plans/AUDIO-FORWARD-STRATEGY.md`.

**The finding that matters most is not a layout finding.** Listeners split hard
over whether an app may trim pauses out of spoken audio. For a podcast app that
is a preference toggle; here the renderer pause grammar is the beat after a
Scripture line, so silence is content. Speed ships, defaults to 1x, and
trim-silence never ships - not as a default, not as a toggle.

**The strategy correction.** The 404 premise was inferred from `git ls-tree` and
is wrong in its consequence: `npm run deploy` builds from the working tree, not
from `HEAD`, so gitignored assets upload like any other file. Spot-checked live
against euangelion.app: `bible-365-day-1`, `-200`, `-365` and
`he-cannot-deny-himself-day-4` all return 200 with `content-type: audio/mp4`.
The catalogue is fully delivered. The real exposure is durability - 380 tracks
exist only on this machine and in the deployed bundle - which is a resilience
project, not a gate.

**A defect neither pass was looking for.** A real GET carrying
`Range: bytes=1000000-1000999` against `/audio/bible-365-day-1.m4a` returns 200
and the entire 7.4 MB body while advertising `accept-ranges: bytes`. Seeking
refetches the whole track. Diagnosis is deliberately out of scope here; it is
recorded so that queue and download work does not get built on top of it.

**The code change is small and is the only runtime change in this feature.**
`is-wide-only` comes off the forward button so both skip directions exist at
every breakpoint; the time readout is what yields when space is tight, moving
its hide threshold from 460px to 560px. The dead `.is-wide-only` rule is removed
rather than left behind.

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- The scroll mini bar shows back-15, play/pause, forward-15, the remaining-time
  readout, and the route back to the full panel.
- The chapter sheet remains bottom-anchored at every width, per SA-034.

### Mobile

- Both skip directions are present at every breakpoint down to 320px. Neither is
  conditional on width.
- Below 560px the remaining-time readout is what hides. Controls never are.
- Both skip controls keep a 44px minimum target.
