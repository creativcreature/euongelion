## Solution

**Feature below the fold becomes a news site.** Seven subject desks —
Beginnings, Scripture, The interior life, Under pressure, Identity and work,
Together, Hard questions — each printed as a section front: a lede column
carrying a plate, beside a filling grid of equal thumbnails. Every entry has an
image. Sections are by SUBJECT, never pathway; SA-050 removed pathway from
browse and it does not return through the side door.

**Rack papers are one size.** `flex: 1 1 0` meant the final rail — which holds
one paper, not four — stretched that paper across the whole rack. Fixed basis
now.

**Shelves centre between matched bookends.** Six thematic pairs (lamp, wheat,
fish, anchor, chi-rho, door), the left one mirrored as a real matched pair is,
indexed by shelf so the wall never reshuffles. Eight spines per shelf rather
than twelve, because at the widened sizes twelve overflowed and wrapped inside
its own row — putting the bookends around two lines of books.

**The Daily Edition carries a real paper.** The Practice, a Greek/Hebrew word
study, How to read (the first practical Bible-reading guides, each led by a
plate chosen for its subject), The daily panel, and the prayer list. Rail briefs
gained thumbnails. All of it rotates by day of year, never at random.

**Home is a mark** — a Levantine house with a parapet roof and an arched door,
three strokes, accessible name still "Home".

## The invention line

The founder authorised invention. This PRD records where it applies.

We invent **our own voice**: practices, guides, panel captions, categories of
intercession, lexical glosses. We do not invent **other people's reported
facts**. `DISPATCHES`, `COMMUNITY` and reader `PRAYERS` stay empty and their
sections stay absent, because a dispatch naming a church in a named place, or a
prayer request from a named reader, is a claim about real people with no source
behind it. The prayer list is a weekday pattern of intercession — categories of
need true every week in every place. Nothing in it claims a fact about a person.

## Performance

`next.config` sets `images.unoptimized`, so a library PNG reaches the browser
untouched. The 16 edition plates are webp derivatives in
`public/images/edition/`: 25.1 MB became 2.26 MB.

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- Feature desk: lede column (~37%) beside a `auto-fit` thumbnail grid, so a
  two-entry desk still reads as a finished block.
- Rack: four papers per rail at a fixed basis, identical whatever the rail holds.
- Spines: eight per shelf on one line, `flex-wrap: nowrap`, bookends at both ends.
- `/today`: practice and word study share a ruled band; guides run three abreast;
  the panel sets plate beside caption; petitions run three across.

### Mobile

- Feature desk: lede stacks above its grid, which drops to two columns.
- Rack: one paper per row.
- Spines: spines shrink rather than wrapping; bookends narrow to 30px.
- `/today`: band, guides, panel and petitions all collapse to one column.
