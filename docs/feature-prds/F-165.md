# F-165: The reading's panel is one row

## Metadata

- Feature ID: F-165
- Category: Reading surfaces
- Owner: founder
- Status: shipped
- Decision: SA-120
- Surfaces: `src/components/NarrationPlayer.tsx`, `src/components/audio/AudioDrawer.tsx`

## Problem

The second half of option A, and the half F-164 shipped without.

The founder, looking at the audio sidebar and the reading's own Audio Edition
panel: "both exist and basically are redundant." Both carried speed, ±15
seconds, chapters and a sleep timer; both drove the same element; both showed
the same position. He picked option A from the mockups — "the sidebar becomes
the full player... the reader panel shrinks to a single Listen row: play/pause,
progress, duration. Anything more opens the sidebar."

F-164 built the sidebar half and said plainly that the panel was not slimmed.
This is that.

## What changed

**The panel is one row.** Play/pause centred, the progress control, the time
remaining — and two things beside them: clipping, and the way through to
everything else.

**Clipping stays, deliberately.** It attaches a note to THIS reading at THIS
timestamp; it is an act of reading, not a player control, and it exists nowhere
else. Removing it in the name of "one row" would have deleted a feature under
cover of a layout decision.

**Speed, skip, chapters and the sleep timer leave.** Every one of them already
had a home in the sidebar. That duplication was the thing complained of.

## What moving them exposed

Three faults, none of which would have mattered while the panel still carried
its own working copies. All three are fixed here, test-first.

1. **The sidebar's sleep timer did nothing.** F-164 gave it a chip and a sheet
   that opens, and I called that done. It recorded the choice into component
   state that nothing read. A reader who set fifteen minutes and put the phone
   down got the whole reading — worse than no timer, because they stopped
   watching the clock. It now counts against a wall-clock end time and stops
   the reading, including the end-of-chapter mode.

2. **The fade came with it.** The panel eased the volume down over five seconds
   rather than cutting mid-sentence. That is the right behaviour for a sleep
   timer on a devotional, so it moved to the sidebar rather than being deleted
   with the rest.

3. **The sidebar's skip length was never persisted,** and it opened at 1× and
   15s regardless of what the reader had chosen. Harmless while the panel was
   also a speed control; not harmless once the sidebar is the only one. It now
   reads and writes the same two keys the panel does.

## Contract

### Desktop

- The panel row is: play/pause, progress, time remaining, clip, more.
- "More listening controls" opens the sidebar.
- The play control stays centred — the placement settled in SA-058 — with the
  empty first grid column as its counterweight.

### Mobile

- Same row, same order. Every control keeps its 44px target.
