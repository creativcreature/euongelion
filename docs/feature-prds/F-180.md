## Problem

Founder, 2026-09-08: "the featured devotional should be the latest devotional
added to the site. That should be a standing rule." And: "Give the seeking help
section on the bottom in red."

SA-031 had already ruled the feature slot belonged to the most recent series, in
July. It drifted anyway. Sought and Crowned shipped on 2026-09-05 and the
homepage kept featuring Drawing Near, because the slot was a hand-edited
`HOMEPAGE_TODAY` constant and nothing connected publishing a series to editing
it.

## Solution

**The feature is derived.** `latestFeaturedSeries()` reads
`devotional-publish-dates.ts` — generated from `git log --diff-filter=A`, gated
by a pre-commit check that fails when any devotional lacks a date — and returns
the series with the newest real publication date, shaped for the slot. Title,
question, day-one link, anchor and art all come from `SERIES_DATA`, so the
homepage cannot claim something the series page contradicts.

`first-seen` dates are excluded. They record the day a bulk-import script ran,
not a publication; the register's own header says so. Letting one win would hand
the homepage to whichever legacy file a migration last touched.

Publish dates decide it rather than `NEW_SERIES_ORDER`, which is append-only by
convention rather than enforcement — a series inserted mid-array would silently
take the slot.

**The test is the rule.** `homepage-features-latest-series.test.ts` recomputes
the newest series independently and fails if the homepage disagrees. It also
asserts the copy is real series copy and that the art exists on disk.

**The outreach band is red.** Brand crimson on the rules, kicker, heading and
link, over a 4% tint. Deliberately not a solid crimson field: the standing
direction for this band is that it should read as a door left open, not an alarm
shouted at every reader. The body paragraph keeps normal ink contrast, because
it is the text someone under stress actually has to read.

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- Feature slot renders the newest series; outreach band sits last on the page.

### Mobile

- Same. The band's tint and rules are colour only — no layout change.
