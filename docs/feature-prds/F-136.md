## Problem

The Daily Bread is a newspaper that repeats every ten days. Every "daily"
section is a fixed array in `src/data/daily-edition.ts` rotated by
`dayOfYear % length` — 14 practices, 6 guides, 10 panels, 12 word studies, 7
prayer foci — so a reader who returns for a fortnight has seen the whole paper
twice. New content means editing TypeScript and deploying.

Founder, 2026-08-18:

> "I want to drastically improve The Daily Bread. It needs new content every
> day. The cartoons are VERY bad right now. The devotional needs to be treated
> like a feature article. The entire page needs to lean extremely heavily into
> an Editorial Newspaper Magazine hybrid. More imagery, more content. Should be
> its own daily scroll hole of Good News. … Crosswords, interactives, video
> content, pull in legal and unblocked content from youtube and other christian
> sites, lining back to them. … Very image and text as art forward. Dynamic
> daily. I dont want to have to prompt the content."

And on scope: "Fully locked to the one page tho!"

## Solution

**An edition engine.** One `edition_items` table keyed by `(kind, publish_date,
slot)`, one generator module per kind, one runner that serves both the nightly
GitHub Action and the founder's monthly batch. Content publishes by writing
rows to Supabase — no commit and no deploy, which keeps this project's most
failure-prone operation out of the daily path.

**Most of the paper needs no model.** The daily prayer, word study, crossword,
unscramble, quiz, gallery, liturgical season and commemoration are all computed
from assets already in the repo — the 7-translation Bible corpus, the Strong's
lexicon index, and the print archive. They write in as `approved` because there
is nothing to review in a computed crossword. Only invented voice (practice,
strip captions, the weekly net-new lead) and third-party items reach the queue.

**The paper grows a puzzles page, an arts page and a back page.** Crossword,
verse unscramble and quiz; The Gallery (audited classical prints, framed as
reproductions with attribution) and The Screening Room (allowlisted video and
articles, always linked out); the Daily Prayer, The Cloud of Witnesses, Letters
and Notices.

**The cartoons are replaced, not repaired.** `par-persistent-widow.webp` is a
man in a 1940s fedora at a door in a gothic-spired Western city, gold-lit on a
dark ground; the parable is a widow before a judge. It breaks the locked riso
preamble on subject, period, region, palette and lighting at once. The daily
slot becomes a recurring strip — **The Ninety-Nine** — whose cast was chosen
partly because sheep are simple high-contrast silhouettes with no faces to
drift and no period costume to get wrong, which is the only way an automated
strip survives hundreds of generations.

**The prayer list stops being invented.** F-098's weekday categories were
honest but authored. They are replaced by prayers pulled directly from
scripture, printed in full with their reference. Real people to pray for return
only through sourced, allowlisted feeds with attribution.

## The invention line

F-098 drew it; this keeps it in the same place and tightens it. We invent our
own voice — practices, guides, strip captions, lexical glosses. We do not
invent other people's reported facts. Replacing the authored prayer categories
with scripture removes invention from that section entirely. "On this day in
church history" is explicitly NOT built, because without a sourced dataset it
is precisely what the line forbids.

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- Multi-column editorial spread: the Lead runs as a feature with kicker,
  standfirst, byline, drop cap and pull quotes beside a rail of the edition.
- Puzzles page sets the crossword grid beside its clue columns.
- Gallery prints publish at full plate width with a ruled caption block
  carrying artist, title, date and one paragraph of looking.

### Mobile

- Every band collapses to one column; the Lead keeps its drop cap and pull
  quotes, the rail becomes a horizontal scroller.
- Crossword grid is pinch-free: cell tap opens the clue, with a fixed clue bar
  above the keyboard. Touch targets ≥ 44px.
- Gallery caption stacks beneath its plate.

## Acceptance

- [ ] No section repeats within 30 days except the guides
- [ ] A fresh edition appears daily with no human action and no deploy
- [ ] `--days=30` produces a reviewable month in one run
- [ ] Nothing third-party renders without `source_name` and `source_url`
- [ ] A database failure renders a visible error, never a fallback paper
- [ ] Every strip panel passes the riso preamble: cobalt on cream, Ben-Day dots
      carrying every tone, no greys, no gold, no glow, no text
- [ ] No Gallery print with text artifacts ever publishes
- [ ] The dead `admin/youtube-allowlist` and `admin/feed-controls` mockups are gone
