## User Expectation

The founder is the only reader of this surface. The expectation, in their own
words (2026-08-28): _"you just talking about visuals doesnt do anything —
literally just wasted tokens, I need to literally use the thing to tell if its
good or not."_

So: nine proposed presentation moves, each rendered as working code with its
controls exposed, each with an approve / reject / park control and a comment
box, on one page.

## 10/10 Definition

1. No move is described where it could be demonstrated.
2. Every move responds to input — a slider, a toggle, a pointer, a key.
3. A verdict left on the page is durable and lands in the archive the founder

4. Light and dark both work; 390px does not overflow.
5. Nothing here is reachable by a reader.

## Device Contracts

### Desktop

- Nine move cards in a single column, max 900px measure.
- Pointer-driven moves (ghosted wordmark parallax, cross pile shove, diagram
  callout hover) are desktop-primary and degrade to tap on touch.
- The jump nav lists all nine with bracketed indices.

### Mobile

- 390px: no horizontal overflow on any move (verified).
- The panelled desk collapses its rail to a row of chips and stacks its panes.
- Every interactive control is tap-driven; `navigator.vibrate` fires a short
  tick on state changes where the platform supports it.
- Canvas moves cap DPR at 2 and resample only on resize, not per frame.

## Risks and Rollback

- **Risk:** a canvas move on a low-end phone. Mitigated — the halftone
  resamples only on resize and the `resolve` mode settles and stops.
- **Rollback:** delete `src/components/lab/` and the futures route. Nothing
  else imports them; no reader-facing surface changes.
