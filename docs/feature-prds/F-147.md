## Problem

Founder, 2026-08-19: _"Complete stages 6-9 consecutively without stopping."_

- **Stage 6 — the Edition voiced.** The Daily Bread's reading had audio (all 15
  rotation slugs were rendered under SA-092) but no way to play it from the
  paper itself.
- **Stage 7 — discovery.** Reading has an organising question and a surface that
  asks it. Listening had neither.
- **Stage 8 — new formats.** The Office and Night. See the honest note below.
- **Stage 9 — downloads.** The one listening feature with no reading equivalent.

## Solution

**Stage 6.** `/daily-bread` offers Listen alongside OPEN FULL READER, built on
the server from the manifest and handed to the client as a plain object. It
renders nothing on a day whose reading has no track, rather than a control that
would play silence.

**Stage 7 — the occasion picker.** The listening equivalent of the Soul Audit,
and deliberately faster: two taps, no questionnaire, because someone reaching
for audio usually has their hands full. _How long have you got?_ and _what are
you doing?_ resolve to a queue you press play on, not a list you browse.

Three decisions inside it are worth naming:

- **The answer is stable for the day.** Seeded on the date plus the occasion, so
  going back and choosing again does not silently reshuffle something a listener
  was part-way through.
- **Activity shapes the fill, not the content.** A commute has a hard edge, so it
  under-fills; background listening tolerates overshoot and a longer single
  piece; resting caps piece length so stopping is easy. It never judges which
  content is "suitable", which the catalogue cannot support.
- **It spreads across series before repeating one**, so a 20-minute session is
  not two days of the same arc.

**Stage 9 — downloads.** A service-worker cache the reader fills deliberately.
Two things make it work, and both are traps that were documented before they
were hit:

- **The bucket is not versioned and is exempted from BOTH cache sweeps** — the
  worker's `activate` purge and the client's version-mismatch clear. Each
  matches on the `euangelion-` prefix and runs on every release, so without the
  exemption a reader who saved a series for a flight would lose it to a deploy
  they never saw. A test pins the agreement, because these two files have
  already drifted once (`SW_VERSION` sat nine releases behind `CACHE_NAME`).
- **The worker synthesises 206 from cached bytes.** A cached Response is a 200
  carrying the whole body; handing that to a media element that asked for a
  range makes iOS refuse to seek. Offline gets the same partial-content contract
  as online (SA-098), including a real 416 for an unsatisfiable range.

## Stage 8, honestly: not built

The Office and Night are new writing and new recording, not engineering. I can
build the pipeline; I cannot author devotional content, and inventing liturgy to
close a plan item would be the wrong kind of finished.

What did land against the same gap: the picker's _an hour or more_ assembles a
long session out of existing readings rather than returning nothing, so the
top of the length range is usable today even though nothing single runs past 28
minutes. The gap itself is unchanged and stays visible.

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- `/series` carries the picker above the shelves; `/daily-bread` carries Listen
  beside the reader link.
- Downloads appear per row in the library queue.

### Mobile

- Every control keeps a 44px target.
- Downloads survive deploys, which matters most here — the offline case is a
  phone on a plane, not a laptop.
- The worker answers ranges offline, so seeking a downloaded reading works on
  iOS rather than silently refusing.
