# F-145: The Library says where you are, not just what you own

## Problem

Founder, 2026-08-19: _"The Library needs better organization, and layout. Its as
basic as it can possibly be and needs to be fully addressed."_

The four sections were already right — ACTIVE / SAVED / PAUSED / COMPLETED. The
**cards** were not. A saved series read:

> Whole series · 7 days

and stopped — **identical whether the reader had finished six days or never
opened it**. The one place that should know where you are knew only what you
owned. That is a shelf, not a library.

## Pattern research

Mobbin, iOS:

- **[Coursera](https://mobbin.com/screens/6ce3ea63-ec71-461f-8d5b-58c8633c6cae)** — every card names the next item and links straight to it, so resuming never means landing at the top and hunting.
- **[Speak](https://mobbin.com/screens/9d2d3577-07a1-45db-acf1-42dc269d0216)** — badges the live course `CURRENT`.
- **[Peloton](https://mobbin.com/screens/68ca5c7e-c255-48b4-baf3-b037981cc893)** / **[Moonly](https://mobbin.com/screens/1329e4d1-7a9a-470c-bc4d-a12e9702a3da)** — a completion fraction on the card; In Progress leads the list.

## Fix

`seriesStanding()` derives `{completed, total, percentage, next}` from the local
completion log — a pure derivation over `SERIES_DATA`, no new plumbing, re-derived
每 render so it is correct the moment a day is marked read.

- **ACTIVE** — badged `CURRENT`, with its standing.
- **SAVED** — a started series shows "N of M read" and offers **CONTINUE · DAY n**
  linking to that exact day. `ACTIVATE SERIES` is the right verb for a series
  never opened and the wrong one for a series the reader is six days into, so
  both are offered and the reader picks.
- **PAUSED** — standing alongside the existing "You reached Day N".
- **SAVED order** — started series first. A shelf sorted by save-date answers a
  question nobody asks.

**COMPLETED deliberately unchanged.** A bar that is always full is decoration,
and finished is finished; the completion date is the useful fact.

Rendered with inline design tokens rather than new CSS classes, because
`globals.css` was being actively worked in another session at the time.

## Verified

`__tests__/library-series-standing.test.tsx`, mounting the real `LibraryView`:

- the live series is badged `CURRENT` and shows "2 of 5 read"
- a started saved series offers `CONTINUE · DAY 3`, and the href resolves to
  that day rather than the series top
- an untouched saved series gets no CONTINUE and sorts below a started one —
  the fixture deliberately supplies them in the wrong order so the sort is
  proven rather than accidental

Type-check and lint clean; 200 test files / 2,485 tests green.
