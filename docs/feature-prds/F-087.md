## Correction of record

This shipped first as a **single reading**. That was the assistant's inference
from the phrasing of the request, and it was wrong: `/devo-go` lists day count
among its required inputs and instructs the operator to lock the week shape
before writing anything. The assistant skipped that gate and then wrote the
choice into `production-decisions.yaml` as though it were policy. The founder
corrected it the same day and chose a clean-sheet rebuild of all seven days.

The stated reason for the one-day call — that the delivery date allowed only one
reading — did not hold either. The commitment was for **Monday**; the correction
came on **Friday**. See SA-036, which now records the reversal rather than the
original claim.

The research and source pack carried over intact, which is why the rebuild cost
writing rather than verification.

## User Expectation

The founder promised a colleague a series on a named theme by a named date. She
asked for **healing** and **being patient**; he added **rest**. He then spoke a
five-minute reading on the spot. The expectation is that what he said arrives
written down at full production quality, with a link he can send.

Two audiences, one artifact:

1. **The person it was written for** — worn down, in a season that has taken
   capacity, and inclined to read a hard season as something being done _to_ her.
2. **Every other reader** — the series is in the public catalog, so it cannot
   depend on knowing who it was for, and must not name or describe her.

## Content design

**The lexical architecture** is the teaching payload, and every entry is verified:

- _qavah_ (H6960) — to wait, lean toward. BDB's twist/bind derivation carried
  with BDB's own "perhaps."
- _chalaph_ (H2498) — root sense "to slide by, pass on… or change"; causative in
  Isaiah 40:31, rendered "change for better, renew." Renewal is not a refill.
- _ogkos_ (G3591) — bulk, mass. No moral charge. Weight is not sin.
- _euperistatos_ (G2139) — hapax legomenon; Thayer's notes it is "not found
  elsewhere," so every rendering is a reconstruction.
- _hypomonē_ (G5281) — patience (KJV) and endurance (modern) are one word;
  _hypo_ + _menō_, to stay under.
- _rothem_ (H7574) — the desert broom, _Retama raetam_; four OT occurrences,
  including Job 30:4 (roots eaten by the destitute) and Psalm 120:4 (coals as
  judgment).
- _demamah_ (H1827) — silence, stillness; _qol demamah daqqah_ is closer to "a
  voice of thin silence," and translators have never converged.
- _perispaō_ (G4049) — to drag all around; one NT occurrence, Luke 10:40,
  **imperfect passive** — done to Martha, not chosen by her.
- _meris_ (G3310) — an allotted share; the same word Colossians 1:12 uses of the
  saints' inheritance, which is why "shall not be taken away" is legal language.
- _aphoraō_ (G872) — to look away from all else at one object; present
  participle, so continuous.
- _archēgos_ (G747) — file-leader, the first in a procession. _teleiōtēs_
  (G5051) — a second hapax, in the verse after the first one.

**Where it resolves.** The risk was writing a try-harder week to someone who
asked for rest. Isaiah puts the running after the waiting; Elijah gets two meals
and two sleeps before a word; Milton answers the day-labour complaint with "God
doth not need either man's work or his own gifts." Rest is what the race is run
on.

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- `/series/looking-at-the-sun` renders the hero plate and all seven days in the
  standard series layout.
- Each `/devotional/looking-at-the-sun-day-N` renders its full module stack; the
  two-minute open occupies sections 1-6 on days 1-6 and the DEEP DIVE CTA jumps
  to section 7. Day 7 has no open — it is the sabbath short form.
- `bleed` inline images span the reading column's full bleed width; `narrow`
  images sit inside the prose measure. Every caption is visible — the caption is
  the contextual justification for the image being used at all.
- Prev/next day navigation works across all seven days.
- The narration player attaches per day from `src/data/audio-manifest.json`, with
  a chapter sheet built from that day's own headings.

### Mobile

- Tested at 375px, 768px and 1024px. Reading column, pullquotes and all inline
  images stay within the viewport with no horizontal scroll.
- Touch targets on the CTA, chapter sheet and transport stay ≥ 44px.
- The two-minute open is the mobile-first contract: reachable without a chapter
  jump, and complete on its own for a reader on a phone.
- Narration plays through a real `<audio>` element, so it survives the screen
  sleeping and appears on the lock screen.
