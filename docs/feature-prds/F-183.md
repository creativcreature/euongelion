## Problem

Founder-directed 2026-09-12: "This weeks theme is Romans 14 vs Leviticus 11- Law vs
Freedom. The entire week should revolve around what is freedom in Christ- what is
respect for Christ sacrifice, while recieving Christ inheritence. Clasping his heel
like Jacob and Essau and inheritence and blessing through his broken slain body."
On stance: "I want to deeply explore all sides of what this means. I dont want to
enter into this biased at all." On the writing: last week's prose was rejected as
mannered, and the SA-138 rule was restated for this run.

## Solution

Seven days, Monday start (SA-123 shape), image intensity 1 across every plate, about
five plates per teaching day read in order as one picture story.

Jewish readings of Leviticus 11, Catholic, Orthodox, Reformed, Lutheran, Methodist,
new covenant, dispensational, Seventh-day Adventist, Messianic Jewish and One Law.
