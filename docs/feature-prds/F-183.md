## Problem

Founder-directed 2026-09-12: "This weeks theme is Romans 14 vs Leviticus 11- Law vs
Freedom. The entire week should revolve around what is freedom in Christ- what is
respect for Christ sacrifice, while recieving Christ inheritence. Clasping his heel
like Jacob and Essau and inheritence and blessing through his broken slain body."
On stance: "I want to deeply explore all sides of what this means. I dont want to
enter into this biased at all." On the writing: last week's prose was rejected as
mannered, and the SA-138 rule was restated for this run.

## Solution

Seven days, Monday start (SA-123 shape), image intensity 1 across every plate, about
five plates per teaching day read in order as one picture story.

| Day | Pos     | Title                           | Anchor             |
| --- | ------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| 1   | A       | Be Holy, Because I Am Holy      | Leviticus 11:44-45 |
| 2   | B       | Sold For A Single Meal          | Genesis 25:29-34   |
| 3   | C       | Through The Curtain Of His Body | Hebrews 10:19-22   |
| 4   | B′      | Who Are You To Judge?           | Romans 14:1-4      |
| 5   | A′      | I Will Not Let You Go           | Genesis 32:24-28   |
| 6   | recap   | Called To Freedom               | Galatians 5:13-14  |
| 7   | sabbath | An Easy Yoke                    | Matthew 11:28-30   |

Every position on the food laws is stated in its own terms, agreements first:
Jewish readings of Leviticus 11, Catholic, Orthodox, Reformed, Lutheran, Methodist,
new covenant, dispensational, Seventh-day Adventist, Messianic Jewish and One Law.

### Narration (SA-141)

Founder, mid-build: _"Ensure the narration reads all the words… All excercises should
be read… the entire devotional needs to be read naturally"_, _"I never want to
re-render audio"_ and _"Do not change older devotionals."_ This series is the first
rendered with reading contract 2: every list the page renders is read, citations in
prose are expanded, requests are cut at paragraph and sentence boundaries, and the
script is proved on text (preflight + proofread) before one render and proved from a
transcript after it. Contract 1 is unchanged for every existing track.
