## Problem

Founder, 2026-08-16:

> "spine the books are too thick and type still too small.. i think the titles
> have too much padding or something. the book ends needs to be generated iconic
> art or something. The icons are not good looking. I want modeled thought out
> book ends, and 7 books per row. On List- makle the far left column bold. I
> like ROse, but not sure it can grow with more series being added."

## Solution

**Bookends are generated art.** Seven riso plates of actual carved-stone
bookends — lion, lamb, lampstand, wheat, olive, anchor, fish — each a real
object with a plinth, a back-plate and weight, generated as one consistent
carving series in the locked brand style (cobalt on cream, heavy Ben-Day
halftone, no greys, no text). The library was checked first per the image rules
and holds no cut-out bookend forms, only full scenes with grounds.

**Spines: thinner, bigger type.** Width 132–200px → 78–134px; type 1.45 →
1.72rem. Seven per row stands.

**List takes a bold first column** — the title is the column you scan down.

**The rose window now grows.** Ring capacities were the literal list
`[12, 24, 36]` with a fixed 16% radial step, so a fourth ring landed past the
rim and a fifth had nowhere to go. The geometry is derived from the count
instead.

## Three things worth keeping

**Cutting out halftone art.** A per-pixel colour key punches holes through the
halftone, because the cream *between* dots looks exactly like the cream
background. The mask is built by flood-filling from the border on a **blurred
greyscale copy** — which bridges the dots — then keeping only the **largest
connected component**, which drops the faint haze islands the generator leaves
above the object.

**"Too much padding" was not padding.** `justify-content: space-between` pinned
the title to the top of the spine and the day count to the foot, so every short
title floated above a dead gap. The title centres now; the count is out of flow.

**What actually clipped the titles** was a hardcoded `max-height: 300px` on
`.spine-title`, predating the larger type. In vertical writing mode the *inline*
axis is vertical, so `max-height` caps **line length** — it was ellipsising
titles while the spine still had hundreds of pixels spare. Two rounds of raising
the spine height did nothing because they were not the constraint.

## Contract

- Bookend plates are cut out to transparency. They must never carry a coloured
  ground, or they will print a cream rectangle into dark mode.
- Rose geometry is derived from the catalog count. Nothing is hardcoded to 37.

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- Spines: seven per shelf, 78–134px wide, 620px tall, titles at 1.72rem.
- Bookends: 150px wide, aligned to the foot of the shelf, mirrored as a pair.
- Rose: panes shrink in bands (14% ≤24 series, down to 5% above 160).

### Mobile

- Bookends narrow to 74px; spine titles drop to 1.1rem.
- Rose unrolls to a list under 700px.
