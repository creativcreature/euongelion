## Problem

> "get matched should be a smaller cta. remove the pills on the homeopage
> version as well."

## 1. The CTA was a banner, not a button

`/soul-audit`'s Get Matched ran `w-full px-10 py-5` — the full width of the
680px column and 20px of vertical padding. At that size it stopped reading as
something you press and started reading as a section divider.

It is `w-fit px-8 py-3.5` now: sized to its own label, with the padding brought
down but still comfortably above the 44px touch minimum.

**The centring detail worth keeping — and I got it wrong once.** `cta-major`
sets `display: inline-flex` from a **layered** rule that beats Tailwind's `flex`
utility, and `margin-inline: auto` does nothing to an inline-level box. So the
first attempt (`mx-auto flex w-fit` on the button) left it at 483px from the
left against a 984px right gap — measured, not assumed. The button is centred
**from its parent** now (`<div className="flex justify-center">`), which works
whatever the button's own `display` resolves to. Same class of bug as F-116, one
element away.

The homepage CTA was already inline (`mock-btn-inline`) and is untouched.

## 2. The homepage prompt pills are removed

Four sample-prompt buttons sat under the homepage field — _"I feel anxious about
my future"_, _"I'm doubting everything I thought I believed"_, _"I keep falling
into the same sin"_, _"I don't know what I believe"_ — shown only while the
field was empty.

They are gone. The placeholder (_"Lately, I've been…"_) already models what to
write, and a Soul Audit works on what the reader actually names; offering four
pre-written answers puts words in their mouth before they have found their own.

The pill CSS is **left in place**, unused. Removing it would touch shared
stylesheet regions while other sessions are working in the same file, and dead
rules cost nothing. Worth deleting in a quiet pass.

No test asserted the pills; the three test files containing those phrases use
them as retrieval fixtures, not UI assertions.

## Contracts

### Desktop

- Get Matched is sized to its label and centred in the column.
- The homepage field shows no prompt pills in any state.

### Mobile

- Get Matched keeps a ≥44px touch target at 390px.
- Removing the pills shortens the homepage fold; no layout gap is left behind.
