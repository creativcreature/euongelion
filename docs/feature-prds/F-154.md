# F-154: Listening from where you are, on /today

## Metadata

- Feature ID: F-154
- Category: Reading surfaces
- Owner: founder
- Status: wip
- Decision: SA-108
- Surfaces: `/today` (`CuratedActiveView`), `src/lib/audio/queue-builder.ts`

## Problem

`/today` means **your** reading — your place in a series, or your Soul Audit
plan. It was the last major reading surface with no way to listen, so a reader
who had started a series had to go back to the series page to play it.

## Solution

**`Listen from here` on a curated active series.** The queue begins at the day
on screen and runs to the end. `queueFromDay` drops the days already behind the
reader rather than skipping past them with a cursor — offering to replay day one
when someone is on day four is a different product from the one `/today`
describes.

Two behaviours are deliberate and covered by test:

- An **unnarrated day mid-series does not silence everything after it**: when
  the named day has no track the whole series is returned rather than nothing.
- The control **renders nothing at all** when no remaining day is narrated,
  rather than offering a button that plays silence.

## The plan path has no audio, and should not pretend to

`/today`'s other branch is a Soul Audit plan, and it gets **no Listen control**.
That is a finding, not an omission.

A plan day's content is `DayContent` — generated per reader by
`curated-builder` out of the curation engine, reference volumes and series
metadata. It carries no devotional slug and is not lifted from any file in
`public/devotionals/`. Nothing has ever narrated it, so there is no track to
play.

The tempting move is to offer the audio of the series the plan was _drawn from_
(`PlanRecord.series_slug`). That would play **different words than the ones on
the screen**, which is worse than no button: a reader would follow along and
find the voice reading something else. Rendering audio on demand for generated
days is open question #6 in the engine research and stays deferred.

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- The control sits in the existing action row beside SAVE SERIES and VIEW WHOLE
  SERIES, not as a new band.

### Mobile

- Inherits `ListenButton`'s 44px target and states the remaining runtime, so a
  reader knows what they are committing to before it starts.

## Acceptance

- [x] `Listen from here` on a curated active series, starting at today's day.
- [x] Earlier days dropped, not skipped.
- [x] Whole series returned when the named day has no track.
- [x] Nothing rendered when no remaining day is narrated.
- [x] Plan path deliberately has no control; reason recorded above.
- [ ] Audio for generated plan days — deferred, needs on-demand rendering.
