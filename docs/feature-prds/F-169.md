## User Expectation

- Someone in trouble in Georgia is handed a URL and finds, without signing up
  or reading a devotional first, a phone number that will be answered.
- An older reader can read the page without squinting.
- The page can be printed and handed to someone with no phone at all.

## 10/10 Definition

- Every number and URL on the page is verified against the provider's own site
  or the responsible state agency, and the page says when.
- Where a resource has a catch — a waiting list, an income limit, a religious
  participation requirement, a facility that is not what it appears to be —
  the page says so on the card, before the reader spends a bus fare.
- Faith-based providers are listed as equals inside the practical categories,
  never as the preferred door, and never as a condition of receiving help.
- The page works on a five-year-old phone on a weak connection with JS off.

## Device Contracts

### Desktop

- Two-column resource grid at ≥960px; triage grid three-up at ≥1060px.
- Section plates band to a max 330px height so cards stay above the fold.

### Mobile

- Single column throughout. Emergency band and the PDF link both above the
  fold at 375×812 (PDF measured at 778px).
- No tap target under 44px; phone numbers are 64px-tall press targets.

## Scope

### In scope

- `/seeking-help-georgia` route, 96 vetted entries across 16 categories.
- `src/data/georgia-help.ts` as single source of truth for page and PDF.
- Printable PDF via `npm run build:georgia-help-pdf`.
- 16 riso duotone section plates.
- Links from footer, `/help`, homepage, sitemap, Soul Audit crisis interstitial.

### Out of scope

- Any tracking of who clicks what. This is not a funnel.
- User accounts, saving, or personalisation of the list.
- States other than Georgia (the data shape supports it; the content does not).

## Risks and Rollback

- **Stale numbers are the real risk.** Programmes close and funds run out. The
  page stamps a verification date and names 211 as the live fallback. Re-verify
  before each release and bump `LAST_VERIFIED`.
- **Coverage leans metro Atlanta**, because that is where the services are.
  Rural south Georgia is thinner and the page says so.
- Rollback: delete the route and the five link sites. No shared surface changes
  behaviour, so removal is inert.
