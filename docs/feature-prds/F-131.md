## Shipped — backlog #18

The full panel's meta read `0:00 / 6:37 · 1 of 10 · 0:03 left`, where the
trailing "left" was the **chapter's** remainder. Two time values meaning
different things, and the smaller one made a ten-minute reading look nearly
over.

[Spotify](https://mobbin.com/screens/c9cb0602-c90a-4e10-9e03-14be4407c20c)
reduces the same idea to "3hrs 43min left · 1% complete" — the number a reader
actually decides on at 6am. It now reads `10:00 left · 1 of 3 · 0% complete`.

The docked mini-player in this same component **already counted down**; the full
panel now agrees with it instead of contradicting it.

Two tests encoded the old `elapsed / total` contract and were updated, keeping
the property each was guarding — that the meta is one element, not split, and
that the value is unambiguously queryable.

### #30 is the one worth reading twice

I built a `WeekDots` component before checking, and **reverted it entirely**.
`PresenceWeekRow` already existed under a **founder-locked** contract that is
stricter than what I wrote:

> Lit/unlit only — NO visible numbers, NO "streak", NO "missed", NO red. The
> only count anywhere is the screen-reader summary in `aria-label` —
> accessibility, not gamification.

Mine rendered a visible "2 days of reading this week." That is a direct
violation of the lock, and it would have shipped a second presence widget onto a
page that already had one. Deleted: component, test, CSS and placement, with
both touched files restored byte-for-byte to `HEAD`.

**The lesson, and it is the second time this week:** audit the codebase before
building from a pattern list. The backlog was drawn from a Mobbin scan and
verified defects; the Mobbin-derived half was never checked against what already
existed, so it overstated the gap.
