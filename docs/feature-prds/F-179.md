| Day | Pos     | Title                          | Anchor                |

| 1   | A       | Is There Anyone Left           | 2 Samuel 9:1-4        |
| 2   | B       | What the Breach Costs          | 2 Corinthians 5:18-21 |
| 3   | C       | The God Who Comes Down Looking | Ezekiel 34:11-16      |
| 4   | B′      | Robe, Ring, Sandals            | Luke 15:20-24         |
| 5   | A′      | At the Table, Still Lame       | Ephesians 2:4-7       |
| 6   | recap   | The Week, Gathered Up          | 2 Samuel 9:13         |
| 7   | sabbath | Quiet                          | Zephaniah 3:17        |

The spine is Mephibosheth (2 Samuel 9). The week turns on one rare word: the text
does not call him lame with the ordinary adjective when it introduces him. It uses
נְכֵה _nekeh_, H5223, "stricken" — which occurs three times in the Hebrew Bible.
Twice of Mephibosheth, and once at Isaiah 66:2, of the one God says He will look
at. The word for what made him unpresentable is the word for who God attends to.
