## Problem

Two visual registers now exist and both are wanted. The shipped site imagery is
light — airy, ambient, subject small in a quiet cream frame. The Drawing Near
work established a second, tenebrist baroque register that the founder approved
for video, gifs and motion. Founder, 2026-08-29: _"I actually want to work with
both styles... The baroque is for video, gifs, and motion. The current site style
is for strictly imagery."_

Neither a swap nor a fork was wanted. The ask was one control: _"I should be able
to set an image intensity 1-10, and the image produce should be along a scale
from current site (1) to more cinematic (10)."_ After seeing the ten-step
spectrum the founder chose the five-step collapse.

## Solution

`--intensity=1..5` on `build-prompts.mjs`, inserted between the preamble and the
subject line, defaulting to 1 for stills and 5 for motion (`--motion`).

Each rung ships the **verbatim prompt wording that produced its founder-approved
plate**, held as `## INTENSITY_1` … `## INTENSITY_5` in `prompt-preamble.md` and
extracted by the same section reader the preamble already used. The approved
plates are on disk at `imagery-staging/intensity-spectrum/scale-5/` and are the
specification when a rung is ambiguous.

| Rung | Register            | Deep mass (<64) | Lit (>200) |
| ---- | ------------------- | --------------- | ---------- |
| 1    | airy — current site | 2%              | 79%        |
| 2    | lit                 | 24%             | 36%        |
| 3    | balanced            | 40%             | 23%        |
| 4    | dramatic            | 49%             | 7%         |
| 5    | tenebrist — motion  | 79%             | 1%         |

The intensity block states that it **outranks any ink percentage still written
into a subject line**, which is why adopting this required no rewrite of the
founder-approved `series-image-subjects.json`.

## Why it is a loop and not a setting

Calibrated across 28 generations on one subject. Three findings, each of which
cost a round:

1. **State the cream share, not the ink share.** Asking for "18–35% ink" produced
   a binary result — rungs 1–2 light, 3–10 all maximally dark and
   indistinguishable. The model steers on _"about 36% of this frame remains quiet
   warm cream paper"_.
2. **Say what the reference image is for.** The attached anchor was itself a
   maximum-intensity plate; unqualified it dragged every rung to its own
   darkness.
3. **Name both budgets and forbid both failure directions**, or the model runs to
   whichever end it prefers.

Requested percentages do **not** track linearly with results — one prompt asking
for more deep ink produced a lighter plate than one asking for less. So
`verify-masters.mjs --intensity=N` reports each plate's measured rung against the
requested one, and anything more than one rung off is regenerated. Never
paraphrase an intensity block; the wording is the validated artifact.

## Contract

### Desktop

Unchanged at the reader surface. This is a generation-time control; installed
plate paths, sizes and slots are exactly as before. A series generated at any
rung installs to `public/images/site/series/<slug>.webp` as it always did.

### Mobile

Unchanged at the reader surface. Note for future work: high-rung plates carry
very little lit area, so any future overlay of light type on a rung-4 or rung-5
plate needs its own contrast check — the existing plates were legible because
they were light, not because anything guaranteed it.

## Regression guarded

`verify-masters.mjs`'s SA-052 blank-paper check sampled only light cells and
asked what share was featureless. On a rung-5 plate the lit passages are ~1% of
the frame and are legitimately smooth glow, so the check failed a correct
tenebrist plate. It is now gated on having a measurable lit sample (≥5% of frame)
and reports `n/a` rather than failing. Verified: all five approved rungs pass
against their own number, and the airy end is still judged normally.
