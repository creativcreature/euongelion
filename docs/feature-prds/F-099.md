## Solution

**Feature allocates by weight.** The fix for "unbalanced" is not to even the
sizes out — it is to make them mean something. A front page gives a story column
inches in proportion to its weight; here weight is the length of the reading.
Sorted by days descending, recency breaking ties, lead bylined "The long read".
Setting a 365-day companion as a one-line brief while a five-day ran across the
top was exactly the nonsense described. The asymmetry stays; it now tells the
truth about scale.

**Seven spines to a shelf, larger titles.** Seven buys ~30px of spine width,
spent on type (1.12rem → 1.45rem). Spine height 380 → 515px, because at the
larger size long titles clipped mid-word — on a spine that reads as a printing
fault. Anything that ever outgrows its spine ellipsises rather than being cut.

**Issues takes masthead scale.** An issue row is a masthead line, so its title
moves off body scale onto `clamp(1.5rem, 2.3vw, 2.25rem)`.

**Mosaic becomes the rose window.** Every other view in the set is a rectangle —
grid, rail, shelf, list — so this one leaves the rectangle. A rose window is the
one piece of furniture a church puts its whole story into, and it is built the
way a catalog is: a centre, then rings working outward, each pane its own scene,
legible only when the light is behind it.

The glass is unlit by default. The pane under the cursor lights, and the **hub**
carries its title at readable size — which is how thirty-seven titles are
available without thirty-seven captions fighting each other around the rim.
Nothing spins on load and nothing rotates on hover: a window is still, and this
is a reading surface.

## Contract

- Rings are computed (12, 24, 36, then remainder), never hardcoded to 37. Add a
  series tomorrow and the window still closes.
- Under 700px the window unrolls into a ringed list — outer-ring panes fall
  below the 44px touch minimum on a phone, so the radial form is dropped rather
  than shrunk.
- Rose panes load **eager**, never lazy. Chrome does not fire its lazy loader
  for them: the panes are transformed off their grid position with
  `translate(-50%, -50%)`, and every one sat with an empty `currentSrc` and an
  unlit plate while sitting in the middle of the viewport. `Plate` gained an
  `eager` prop that sets `loading="eager"` **without** the preload hint
  `priority` adds — thirty-six preloads would compete with the page.
- `statusLabel` already falls back to `dayCountLabel`. Never pair them; doing so
  printed "6 DAYS · 6 DAYS".

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- Feature: day counts never increase reading down the page (lead → stories →
  briefs).
- Spines: seven per shelf, one line, 515px tall, titles at 1.45rem.
- Issues: title at masthead scale, balanced wrap.
- Rose: 860px square, hub at 30%, rings at 27% / 43% of half-width.

### Mobile

- Spines: titles drop to 1.1rem; spines shrink rather than wrapping.
- Rose: unrolls to a vertical list, hub first, no glass filter, full-width rows.
