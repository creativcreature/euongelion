## Problem

The founder authored a full thematic in `content/next-series-thematic.md` — a
seven-day arc on nearness running from Genesis 1 to Revelation 22, with the day
plan, the Hebrew and Greek, the editorial stance, nine verification flags and an
explicit cuts list already written. It needed building, and it needed building at
the new baroque register rather than the site's usual light one.

## Solution

Seven days, Monday start (SA-123), built to the founder's thematic as the
governing spec.

The week turns on one lexeme. Hebrews 10:1 calls them "the comers thereunto" —
προσέρχομαι, the drawing-near ones, whom the law could not perfect. Verse 22 uses
the same verb as an invitation: "let us draw near." One word, twenty-one verses
apart, with the torn veil between them.

## Verification

Scripture was pulled verbatim from `public/bibles/KJV/` — 89 verses across 43
references — and never from memory. Casing was preserved (this repo's KJV prints
"the Lord"). Every Greek claim in the thematic was checked against the local
tagged Greek New Testament rather than accepted:

| Claim                       | Result                                                                                        |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Matt 5:17 destroy vs fulfil | καταλύω G2647 vs πληρόω G4137 — VERIFIED                                                      |
| John 19:30 τετέλεσται       | G5055 τελέω, perfect passive — VERIFIED                                                       |
| Heb 10:1 / 10:22 same verb  | both G4334 προσέρχομαι — VERIFIED                                                             |
| Heb 10:19 παρρησία          | G3954 — VERIFIED                                                                              |
| Rom 10:4 τέλος              | G5056, glossed goal — VERIFIED                                                                |
| Gal 5:14 (thematic flag 7)  | πληρόω G4137 — VERIFIED; carries a textual variant, so the series does not build on the tense |

Four of the thematic's nine flags changed the text as a result:

1. **Revelation 22:14 is not used.** Confirmed from the corpus that this KJV reads
   "they that do his commandments" — the series closes on 22:2 and 22:4 instead,
   and Day 6 states why in the reader's hearing.
2. **Hosea 6:6 always appears with Mark 12:33.** The comparative control was
   verified verbatim; without it Day 5 would contradict Day 2.
3. **"No other attribute of God is tripled" was softened.** It is a universal
   negative this build did not verify, so Day 2 claims only the throne-room
   visions.
4. **Isaiah 26:3 _shalom shalom_ was cut** — no Hebrew text in the source pack
   confirms the MT reading.
5. **The τετέλεσται receipt claim was cut** and Romans 10:4 leads instead. Day 3
   tells the reader why.

embed-block-checked at build time.

## Imagery — intensity 5 (SA-131/SA-132)

21 plates, three per day, generated at `--intensity=5` per the founder's ruling
that a named intensity applies to every image in the run. Six of the seven
founder-approved Drawing Near baroque plates were reused; fifteen were generated
new and one of those was regenerated for textual accuracy.

**No visible hands in any of the 21.** Hands are handled compositionally — no
figures, backs turned with arms down, or hands cropped outside the frame — because
three prior rounds proved instructing the generator to hide them does not hold.

**One plate was rejected and regenerated on textual accuracy:** the first
`day1-hiding` read as stone pillars, and Genesis 3:8 says they hid "amongst the
trees of the garden." Style-correct and textually wrong is still wrong (SA-124).

All 21 pass `verify-masters.mjs --intensity=5`.
