# F-153: The queue is a drawer, not a bar

## Metadata

- Feature ID: F-153
- Category: Reading surfaces
- Owner: founder
- Status: wip
- Decision: SA-107
- Surfaces: root layout, `/series/[slug]`, `/library`, `/` (homepage), `src/components/audio/*`, `src/stores/playlistsStore.ts`

## Problem

Founder, 2026-08-19: audio was _"clunky on the site in the areas that aren't
strictly the devotional"_, should _"exist as a drawer queue"_, readers should be
able to _"add to the playlist"_ as they navigate and _"store playlists in
library"_, and the whole thing should be _"tucked, but not invisible"_ — with a
callout on the homepage so people know it exists.

What was there was a full-width bar pinned across the bottom of every page. It
announced itself constantly and pushed against the written content the site is
actually for, which is the opposite of the intended posture.

## Solution

**The bar becomes a handle.** `AudioDrawer` replaces `GlobalAudioBar` (deleted,
its contracts carried across into the new tests). Collapsed, it is a compact
pill on one side carrying only the title, a play control, and `+N` for what is
behind it. Tapping it brings the queue up as a drawer.

_Not invisible_ is a specific set of decisions, not a slogan: the handle is
present whenever a queue exists; its three bars animate only while audio is
actually sounding, and stop entirely under `prefers-reduced-motion`; and the
count means "there are four more after this" is legible without opening
anything.

**Two sibling buttons, not one nested in the other.** The first draft put the
play control inside the expand button. A control inside a control is invalid
markup and unreachable by keyboard.

**Adding is quiet.** `AddToQueue` is the `+` every studied queue uses on a
browse row. It never starts playback, never navigates, and never takes over from
what is already sounding — and it confirms briefly, because an add with no
feedback reads as a dead button.

On a series day it is a **sibling of the link, never inside it**, positioned in
the cell. A button nested in an anchor is invalid and unreachable, which is why
the earlier pass left this undone rather than forcing it.

**Playlists are kept in the library.** An earlier draft argued against named
playlists at 98 hours of catalogue; the founder overruled it and that ruling
stands. What follows is that saving must be cheap: one tap in the drawer on
whatever is already queued, rather than a create-then-fill flow nobody finishes.
`SavedPlaylists` renders nothing until a reader has saved one.

**The homepage says audio exists.** Tucked with no announcement is just hidden.
The callout leads with the occasion rather than the feature — what a listener
wants to know is that this works while their hands are busy — and states the
catalogue's hours computed from the manifest, so the claim cannot drift from
what actually renders.

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- Handle is right-aligned above the tab bar; the drawer is centred and capped.
- Escape closes the drawer, as every sheet on this site does.

### Mobile

- Handle and every drawer control keep a 44px target.
- The handle caps its width so a long title truncates rather than spanning the
  viewport.
- The drawer is bottom-anchored, which is where a transport belongs.
