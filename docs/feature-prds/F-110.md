# F-110: Flow is an artboard, not a card wall

## Problem

> "Flow is designed nothing like the example fyi."

Correct. It was a bordered gallery widget with a caption scrim on every tile.
The reference (nicolaromei.com) is a **full-bleed artboard of pure imagery**.

Plus, from the same brief: *"do not allow for awkward spaces like this."*

## Rebuilt on four points

1. **Full bleed.** The frame breaks the page measure with `width: 100vw` and
   `margin-inline: calc(50% - 50vw)`, so the tiles *are* the surface rather than
   sitting inside a box.
2. **Tiles butt on a hairline.** A 3px gutter, not 14px — the difference
   between cards on a page and one printed sheet cut into plates.
3. **No permanent captions.** The plates are pure image; the name arrives on
   hover or focus. Kept in the DOM (never injected) so assistive tech always has
   it, and always visible below 900px where there is no hover.
4. **Four tile shapes** rather than two, because a grid of two sizes reads as a
   card wall.

## And the holes

Measured **13 empty cells** in the middle of the board — the exact awkward
spaces called out. Three causes, fixed in order:

1. **Adaptive shape.** A 2-wide tile placed at the lowest *pair* of columns will
   happily skip over a single-column trough, and nothing later fills it. If
   going narrow sits higher, it goes narrow.
2. **Relocation.** Single-cell tiles move up into whatever gaps survive, deepest
   gap first.
3. **Growth.** Any gap that still remains is closed by growing the tile directly
   above it down by one. A slightly taller plate is invisible; a hole is not.

Proven across every column count: **zero holes and zero overlaps at 2, 4, 6 and
8 columns** — the only counts FlowView ships. (3 and 7 retain one hole each and
are never rendered.) `__tests__/flow-packing.test.ts` pins this, plus
determinism and the biggest-tile-for-the-longest-reading rule.

## Contract

- **The plates are eager.** They sit inside a scroll container, and Chrome
  leaves lazy images unloaded there — the board renders as a flat dark field
  with no tiles visible at all. This cost two debugging rounds. Do not make them
  lazy.
- The board carries a slightly lighter ground than the frame, so the cut lines
  between plates read even before imagery paints.

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- 6 columns to 1300px, 8 above. Frame 82vh, full viewport width.
- Caption reveals on hover/focus; a crimson inset marks the active plate.

### Mobile

- 2 columns under 560px, 4 under 900px. Captions always visible.
- Frame is a native scroll container; pointer drag bails on touch.
