## Solution

**Mobile series toggles.** The kiosk bar is hidden under 640px — at one paper
per row it becomes a heavy rule between single papers, the opposite of its
purpose. Papers stop leaning, since a tilt reads as breakage at full width.
Covers drop to one per row at 4:5.

**The rose becomes a lancet.** A rose window is a *wide* wall's opening; a phone
is a narrow wall, and narrow walls get **lancets** — tall single-light windows
set in a row. So the concept is rebuilt rather than shrunk: one mullion runs the
height of the window, lights hang off it alternating side to side, and each
carries its **title beside the glass** — the one thing the desktop wheel cannot
do, which arguably makes the mobile form the more readable of the two.

**Home.** The Bible-365 secondary CTA is gone (one offer, not two). The featured
plate drops from ~525px to a 21:9 banner capped at 300px.

**How we write is footer-only** — out of the desktop bar and the hamburger.

**The paper is a bento sheet.** Every editorial piece below the front page is a
ruled compartment; compartments butt edge to edge sharing rules, which is how a
page is made up and why a newspaper reads as one sheet rather than a stack of
cards. Structural reference: seaharvest.net.au — scoped by the founder to the
Today and Daily Bread pages only, never site-wide.

**Site-wide scroll motion.** Reference for feel: oci.madebybuzzworthy.com.

## The scroll line was invisible for two reasons

Both fixed, and the second is the standing token trap:

1. It sat at `--z-sticky` (100) while the topbar is 120 and the nav 110 — the
   header painted straight over it.
2. It was `--color-gold`, which **is cobalt in light mode**. Even where it
   wasn't covered, it vanished into the header.

It now rides at `z-index: 400` on the very top edge, in `--mock-blue`.

## The motion safety property

This is the whole design of `src/components/motion/Reveal.tsx`:

The pre-reveal hidden state exists **only** under `.motion-ready`, a class the
island adds on mount. No JS → no class → no hidden content, and the page renders
exactly as it does today. Motion can only ever **add** to a working page; it can
never gate one. Reveals fire once and unobserve. `prefers-reduced-motion` stops
the rAF loop from ever starting. Parallax is capped at ±28px so an image never
separates from its caption.

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- Scroll line visible above the header stack at all times.
- Reveal: 18px rise + fade, 620ms, staggered 70ms per sibling to a cap of 6.
- Parallax on the home featured plate, the panel plate and guide plates.

### Mobile

- Rack: no kiosk bar, no lean, one paper per row.
- Covers: one per row, 4:5.
- Rose: lancet — mullion, alternating lights, titles beside the glass.
- Paper compartments stack to one column, keeping their shared rules.
