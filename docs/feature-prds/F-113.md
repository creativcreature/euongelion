## Problem

> "not a fan at all. Change it to a two column version with the left column th
> image and the right column a small 2-3 sentence summary of the devotional.
> Instead of Flow this is Preview. Cover is now the default then preview is the
> next toggle etc."

## Flow is deleted

Component, styles, drag/wheel/drift machinery and tests all go. It went through
**four rebuilds in one day** and never landed.

The reason is worth keeping: every one of those rebuilds was designed from a
*description* of a reference rather than from measuring the reference. The
founder finally had to say *"Please look at the dang site. ACTUALLY LOOK AT
IT."* **Measure the reference before designing against it.**

The brief that replaced it is plainer and better. A reader wants to see the art
and find out what the reading is about — and panning, drifting columns, size
levels and motion blur all stood between them and both of those things.

## Preview

Ruled rows: **plate left in a fixed column, copy right.**

Fixed rather than fractional. At a share of the width the plate grew with the
page and every row ran ~390px tall, which is a lot of scrolling across 37
series. A fixed column holds each row near 210px at any viewport.

Under 700px it stacks to one column — two would leave the summary in a gutter
too narrow to read.

## The summary is real copy

Every series already carries a hand-written `introduction`. Most are already two
or three sentences; three run to six, so the summary prints the **first three** —
the author's own words, in the author's own order, simply stopped early.

Nothing is paraphrased and nothing is generated. There is no CSS line-clamp
either, so nothing is ever cut off mid-word.

Preview is **server-rendered**: it holds no state, so it has no business being a
client island.

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- Toggle order: Covers (default), Preview, Issues, Feature, Rack, Spines, List.
- Plate column `clamp(200px, 24vw, 320px)`, 3:2, cover-cropped.
- Summary measure capped at 62ch; "Read this series" appears on hover/focus.

### Mobile

- Rows stack to one column under 700px; the read-more line is always visible,
  since there is no hover.
