## Problem

Founder, 2026-08-16:

> "I want the audio player to be modeled after the audible audio player, but to
> still be inline and small footprint. The it needs to have proper play buttons,
> chapters, etc. I also want people to be able tot listen at 2x speed etc. The
> current player is too basic. But it has the right amount of footprint (taking
> up the right amount of on screen space) but the type can be smaller and more
> finessed, and the player features need to be better thought out and more
> robust."

The footprint is right. The transport is not. Today it is five uppercase
word-buttons — `−15 / LISTEN / +15 / CHAPTERS / 1×` — a range scrubber, a
chapter sheet and a mini bar. Structurally sound, dressed as an editorial widget
rather than a transport.

Two concrete gaps behind the aesthetic one:

- **2× does not exist.** Speed cycles `0.8 → 1 → 1.25 → 1.5` and nothing else,
  and because it cycles, reaching any value costs up to four taps.
- **Chapters are computed and unused.** All 528 tracks carry measured chapter
  timings (never estimated — `build_chapters.py` refuses to emit a devotional
  whose re-extraction disagrees with what was spoken), yet nothing in the
  transport can step between them.

## Solution

**Glyphs replace word-buttons**, which buys back the space the new controls
need inside the same box height. Play/pause as a filled disc anchor, arc-arrow
skips with the interval drawn inside, chapter prev/next, and a right-hand group
of speed, chapters, sleep and clip.

**A speed sheet, not a cycle** — 0.75 / 1 / 1.25 / 1.5 / 1.75 / **2** / 2.5 / 3,
with `preservesPitch` set so a 2× reading is not chipmunked. Skip interval stays
**15s rather than Audible's 30s**: this prose is dense and many readings run
under five minutes. 30s is offered as a setting.

**Chapter stepping and chapter ticks.** Back goes to the _start of the current
chapter_ unless already within 3s of it, then to the previous one — otherwise
the control is useless for "restart this section", which is the common case. The
scrubber gains tick marks and a `left in chapter` readout.

**A sleep timer** — 5/10/15/30 minutes and end-of-chapter — that fades over 5s
rather than cutting. This is a devotional played at night; a hard stop
mid-sentence is the opposite of what the feature is for.

**Cross-device resume** (migration 018): start on your phone, finish on your
laptop. One upserted row per reader per devotional, deliberately not an
append-only event log — a log would write on every tick for no benefit, and
Workers allows 10ms CPU per request. `seconds_listened` accumulates real
playback time so a later year-in-review can total hours without an events table.

Conflicts resolve by **newest write, never furthest position**. A reader who
deliberately restarts on their phone must not be dragged back to where the
laptop stopped.

## Contracts

### Desktop

- Same panel height as the current transport; no growth in footprint.
- Chapter name in the eyebrow row; elapsed / total and time-left-in-chapter in
  oldstyle numerals.
- Sheets use the `NarrationChapters` idiom: portal, focus trap, Escape to close,
  scroll lock, focus restored on unmount.
- Chapter ticks are `pointer-events: none` so they never steal a scrubber drag.

### Mobile

- Every control keeps a ≥44px touch target at 375px; the control row wraps
  rather than overlapping.
- The mini bar keeps its current height — `globals.css` positions the
  reader-theme button and chat launcher off `--narration-bar-h`, and changing it
  silently re-collides them.
- Tested at 375 / 768 / 1024.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Playback reaches 2× and stays in pitch.
2. Chapter prev/next steps correctly, including the restart-current-chapter
   window.
3. Sleep timer fades rather than cuts, and is suppressed under
   `prefers-reduced-motion`.
4. The scrubber remains an `<input type="range">` — the keyboard and
   screen-reader affordance is not replaced by a div.
5. Position resumes across devices for a signed-in reader; on-device only when
   signed out.
6. Server writes are throttled to at most one per 30s while playing, and flush
   on pause, seek, end and `pagehide`.
7. Until migration 018 is applied, resume degrades to on-device and logs
   `MIGRATION_018_PENDING` — it does not error the reading.
8. `listening_progress` is covered by both data export and account deletion.
