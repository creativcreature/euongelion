## 1. The thematic

Named for ἀναζωπυρεῖν (_anazopyrein_, 2 Timothy 1:6) — a New Testament hapax
legomenon meaning to rekindle, built on ζώπυρον, embers. The small group
carrying the series is itself called Rekindled.

Founder ruling on the engine: "because he would love first, we have
relationship, that is how we should exist, in relationship with Him, and in
community." 1 John 4:19 is the ORDER OF OPERATIONS, not an evangelism tactic.

Differentiation was verified rather than assumed. Four adjacent series already
exist — `abiding-in-his-presence` (John 15 end to end), `too-busy-for-god`
(Ecclesiastes), `present-in-the-chaos` (stillness), `hearing-god-in-the-noise`
(discernment). All four are DISCIPLINE series. This is a COVENANT series, and no
existing series uses fire, lamp, flame, altar or lamb language.

## 2. Three images per day, composed for motion

Founder direction: three plates per day forming a narrative arc (lead, middle,
conclusion), each composed so that exactly ONE element can be animated as a
cinemagraph while everything else stays hard-anchored. Full scope is a storyboard
for future YouTube episodes.

Every subject line therefore carries a named MOTION element, its direction of
travel, and an explicit list of static anchors. Every motion assigned is a
naturally looping phenomenon — flame guttering, smoke curling, heat shimmer,
water lapping, cloth in wind, dust in a light shaft, embers breathing. A moving
figure does not loop; those were excluded deliberately.

## 3. Compositions anchored to real, verified cinema stills

Three prompt generations were rejected before this landed:

- **v1** flat/poster — not composed for motion at all.
- **v2** added abstract "cinematic" language ("three depth planes, committed light
  direction"). Band compliance COLLAPSED from 8/22 to 3/22, because cinematic
  depth staging IS tonal mass. Founder verdict: "too fake looking and fabricated."
- **v3** anchored to famous paintings recalled from memory. Killed before
  generating on the founder's instruction to research actual film stills.
- **v4** anchored to 20 verified cinema shots.

The research screened ~4,500 stills across ~90 films by downloading full-size
frames and measuring them numerically (horizon as a percentage of frame height,
peak-brightness centroid, value separation), because WebSearch was exhausted and
every general engine was CAPTCHA-blocked. Falling back to memory was rejected as
the one unacceptable outcome for a task premised on "verify the shot exists."

Anchors include _Silence_ (Prieto), _Nostalghia_ (Lanci), _The Northman_
(Blaschke), _The Turin Horse_ (Kelemen), _Lawrence of Arabia_ (Young), _Days of
Heaven_ (Almendros), _Satantango_ (Medvigy), _A Man Escaped_ (Burel), _Late
Spring_ (Atsuta), _L'Eclisse_ (Di Venanzo), _Playtime_, _The Others_, _The
Return_, _Dersu Uzala_, _Close Encounters_ (Zsigmond), _Raise the Red Lantern_.

**Two briefs came back honestly unmatched and are marked as adaptations in the
prompts themselves** — a two-pairs-of-hands lamp handover (no such shot exists in
anything screened) and a town rendered as scattered window-lights (an automated
detector across 17 films returned nothing). Neither was faked.

Rejected on the two-ink constraint after being viewed: Blade Runner 2049, The
Revenant, Macbeth 2015, Son of Saul, The Green Knight, Kingdom of Heaven — their
beauty is grading, haze and long tonal ramps, none of which survives a two-colour
halftone.

## 4. Coverage bands recalibrated (supersedes the SA-052 numbers for cinematic work)

The founder's cinematic direction and the existing band gate were in direct
conflict. Bands are recalibrated to **AIRY 25-45%, MID 50-70%, DENSE 75-92%**,
with a hard 92% ceiling, and **standard deviation retained as the real gate**.
Rationale: the rejected 33-plate set measured sd 9.7; the approved set 26.8. This
set measures sd 26.2 — it is not failing the sameness problem the gate exists to
catch, only an absolute threshold the new direction made obsolete.

## 5. Verification that changed the content

- **Genesis 15:17 is a TORCH (_lappid_), not a lamp (_ner_).** KJV's "burning
  lamp" is straining. The series runs torch and lamp as two distinct threads —
  torch is what God does alone, lamp is what we are given to keep — which is more
  accurate and stronger than flattening them.
- **The real spine is one verb**: כָּבָה (_kabah_) in Lev 6:13 ("shall not go
  out"), 1 Sam 3:3 ("not yet gone out") and Isa 42:3 ("will not quench"), rendered
  by σβέννυμι in Matt 25:8. The command and the promise are the same word.
- **The bereshit letter pattern is genuinely attested** — Midrash Aggadah (ed.
  Buber, 1894) Gen 1:1:6 — but it is MEDIEVAL, its own reading is
  circumcision/Sabbath and Torah rather than the Genesis 15 covenant, and the
  Baal HaTurim attribution circulating online is FALSE.
- **Three misattributed quotations were verified dead and excluded**: "prayer
  doesn't change God, it changes me" (Shadowlands, not Lewis), "preach the
  gospel, when necessary use words" (1989 newsletter, not Francis), "light a
  candle rather than curse the darkness" (W. L. Watkinson 1907, not Confucius).
- **Monica corrections**: the famous _Confessions_ V.viii.15 line as usually
  quoted is a paraphrase in no published translation; the bishop of III.xii.21 is
  never named; she accused Augustine and went home rather than following his ship;
  ~17 years of prayer, not thirty; "27 August 387" is a 1969 liturgical date.
