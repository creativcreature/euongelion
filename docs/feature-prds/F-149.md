## Problem

The series page already knew read state. It rendered READ AGAIN on a finished
day and tracked n/m COMPLETE. But **every unread day rendered "READ NOW"**, so
day 1 and day 7 of an untouched series were typographically identical.

The card wall answered "what is in this series" and never "where am I". That is
the difference between a contents page and a plan.

## Solution

One day is marked **START HERE** — the first unread day, resolved through
`nextUnreadDay`, the same primitive the Library card and `/today` use, so the
three surfaces cannot disagree about where the reader stands.

- Finished days read ✓ READ.
- Remaining unread days quiet down to READ.
- Future days are **not** locked beyond the existing founder-disabled `canRead`
  gate. Alan's pattern rings the current step and locks what follows; the ring is
  the useful half, and the lock fights a standing ruling.

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- Exactly one card in a series carries START HERE.
- A finished series marks no current day rather than looping back to day 1.

### Mobile

- The marker is the primary typographic signal on the card wall, not a badge
  competing with the title.

## Acceptance

- [x] An untouched series starts at day 1.
- [x] It advances to the FIRST unread day rather than past the highest read one.
- [x] A gap is honoured — read day 1 and day 3, and the reader is sent back to
      day 2 rather than forward.
- [x] A finished series marks no current day.
- [x] Verified by asserting the primitive's contract rather than re-asserting
      React (`__tests__/series-current-day-marker.test.ts`).
