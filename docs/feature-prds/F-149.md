## Problem

Bible 365 was finished as a manuscript and unfinished as a product. All 365 days
were written — 787,621 words — and then measured against the site's own
contracts they failed on every structural axis:

- **12 modules a day, in identical order, on all 365.** Every devo-go series
  runs 26-28.
- **No `format` field on 365 of 365**, so no Two-Minute Open (SA-030/SA-034). A
  reader who stopped early got a fragment, not a reading.
- **Zero** `inline-image`, `pullquote`, `video` or `interactive` modules.
- **One plate of artwork reused across all 365 days** — the series cover
  illustrating the widow's oil, the Red Sea and the Sermon on the Mount alike.
- **`check-readability.mjs bible-365` FAILED**: 11.2% of sentences at 30+ words
  against a gate of 8%, and **509 sentences over 45 words** against a gate of
  zero.
- **All 365 failed `validate-devotional.mjs` outright** on a missing top-level
  `chiasm_position`. The series had never passed the repo's own gate.
- Narration was `am_michael`, the back-catalogue Kokoro voice.

Measured on production at 390x844, one day was **18,747 px — 22 phone screens**,
and screen one carried no Scripture at all.

## Solution

Rewritten to `two-minute-open-v2`, authored by Codex under a controlled brief
with every gate verified independently rather than taken from its self-report.

**Day 1 carries an ad-hoc deep dive** (founder direction, 2026-08-19): the word
study is `bereshit bara Elohim`, opened in several directions. It holds three
tiers visibly apart, which is the whole discipline of the module:

1. **What the Hebrew says** — grammar and lexicon only, checked against
   `morphhb` and Strong's. `bara` is a Qal perfect third-person masculine
   singular; `Elohim` is plural in form beside it.
2. **What the New Testament does with the beginning** — John 1, Colossians 1,
   Hebrews 1, Revelation 22, corpus-verbatim from `public/bibles/BSB`.
3. **How it has been read** — Rashi's "for the sake of _reshit_"; the letter-name
   _beth_ as house, and the dwelling arc through John 1:14 to Revelation 21:3.

Labelled as reading, never as lexicon. Pictographic-Hebrew, gematria and
hidden-code claims are excluded by brief and verified absent.

**Narration is Chatterbox Turbo through local Voicebox**, never ElevenLabs
(founder ruling, hard gate). The same corpus metered is ~$600-800 _per render_
and the corpus will be re-rendered; local is $0 and therefore re-runnable, which
is what makes the readability pass affordable at all.

## The gap this exposed

`render_chatterbox.py` refuses to write `public/audio/` or the manifest by
design, and `render_kokoro.py --publish` is Kokoro-specific — so a verified
Chatterbox render had no way into the catalogue. `publish_chatterbox.py` is that
step. It re-checks the renderer's gates against the sidecar rather than trusting
them, derives chapters as a running sum over measured part durations replicating
the pause grammar, and holds `flock` across read-and-write on the manifest
because several sessions share this tree.

Since SA-098 audio is served from R2 and stripped from the asset bundle, so
publishing is not finished until `./scripts/upload-audio-to-r2.sh` has run.

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- The open renders as a self-contained reading; the deep dive follows the CTA.
- Chapter marks land on module boundaries and are seekable via the R2 range route.

### Mobile

- Scripture appears on the first screen. The open is the above-fold unit.
- The deep dive is one tap, not twenty-two screens of scroll.
