## Problem

SA-027 approved six access paths; Phase 1 shipped subscription + free
grant. Subscription-averse users and gift recipients had no door:
no one-time purchase, no spendable balance, no redeemable codes.

## User Expectation

- "A single edition, no subscription": buy a small pack once; credits
  never expire; the same held-generation resume works after purchase.
- Redeem a code someone bought for me: "Someone covered your edition."
- Nothing ever double-spends: our failures refund, replays grant once.

## 10/10 Definition

- Durable, journaled balance (`users.generation_credits` + append-only
  `generation_credit_ledger`); grants ONLY via verified Stripe webhooks
  (event-idempotent through a unique ledger index) or the atomic
  `redeem_gift_code` DB function (lock → per-user uniqueness →
  decrement → credit → journal).
- Entitlement order: subscription → free grant → credits; consumption
  is an optimistic-concurrency decrement AFTER all pre-checks, refunded
  on plan-creation failure (same discipline as the free grant).
- Gift codes: ≥16-char unambiguous-alphabet codes, sha256-only at rest,
  plaintext returned once at mint (internal-secret admin route),
  redemption rate-limited 5/hour/IP with no validity oracle.
- Config-driven packs: only packs with a configured Stripe price are
  advertised; labels env-tunable; charged amount always Stripe's.
- Paywall pack card + redeem sheet + balance per the approved pattern
  doc §1 (no POPULAR badges, honest expiry line, cross-link up).

## Methodology References

## Device Contracts

### Desktop

- Pack card in the paywall spread's offer stack; redeem sheet as a
  small centered panel; Editions row on /settings/subscription.

### Mobile

- Pack card in the full-height sheet stack; redeem as a bottom sheet;
  ≥44px targets; no horizontal overflow at 375px.
