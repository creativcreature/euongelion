## User Expectation

- CANONICAL ANGLE (founder ruling 2026-07-28, supersedes the single-angle framing this line carried through v1-v5): the series is centred on **patience for the not-yet-turned** — a reader waiting on someone they love who wants nothing to do with God, learning that the harvest is never called early — held together with **the retired verdict**: you cannot reliably tell wheat from weeds, so stop rendering judgment on the field. Beneath both, in a lesser measure: **evil persists beside good** in the world, the church, and the reader's own heart, and God does not pull the weeds now. All three must be present, in that weighting; the series drifted across five re-angles in two days and the founder's "not sure it has the correct context" read (2026-07-28) traces to this line stating only the third.
- Such a reader opens a 7-day deep dive on the Parable of the Wheat and Weeds (Matthew 13:24-30, 36-43) and finds: verified Greek/Hebrew word studies, the Daniel 12:3 / Malachi 4 Old Testament spine, primary-source faith stories, inline-playing official videos, honest handling of the parable's historical misuses in both directions, and a recap equipping study beyond the series.
- Monday start, Sunday Sabbath close; every non-sabbath day opens with the SA-030 Two-Minute Open (scripture → vocab → reflection → prayer → DEEP DIVE cta).
