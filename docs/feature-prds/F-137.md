## Problem

Founder, 2026-08-18: "also fully impliment accounts."

A read-only audit of the entire auth surface (12 broken findings, 13 missing
pieces, all with file:line evidence) found the flows sound at the core —
magic link + 6-digit OTP + Google, session refresh, onboarding — but the
account was not worth having and, worse, not safe to delete:

- **Deletion and export missed the user's own data.** Post-F-105 (two site
  states), signed-in annotations and bookmarks key `session_token` to the
  auth user id; the privacy helpers swept only anonymous session tokens. Every
  note, highlight and bookmark a signed-in reader made survived "Delete My
  Account" — under copy promising permanent removal — and was absent from
  their own GDPR export. `push_subscriptions` had the identical defect.
- **Sign-up was the weaker twin of sign-in**: Google unconditional and
  first, no Turnstile token, no in-app OTP entry — on the page whose users
  are most exposed to the 2-emails/hour mailer cap.
- **Reading progress never reached the server.** `user_progress` existed,
  unused; the account synced nothing a reader cares about.
- **Billing was device-bound**: the portal needed a localStorage checkout id;
  the stripe_customer_id the (now applied) migrations provide was never used.
- **Loose ends**: `/account` referenced by the tab bar but nonexistent; no
  display name UI; no email change; `onboarding_completed` permanently false;
  the middleware matcher guarded only by a comment.

## Solution

Five workstreams, each built and then adversarially verified (two rounds):

1. **GDPR correctness** — deletion and export cover user-id-keyed rows
   (annotations, session_bookmarks, push_subscriptions); a structural test
   prevents the next user-id-keyed table from drifting; the pre-F-105
   audit-UUID-keyed rows are a DOCUMENTED residual gap, not a hidden one.
2. **Sign-up parity** — flag-gated Google below email, Turnstile token,
   in-page OTP entry, guest escape hatch; Turnstile remount defect fixed in
   both twins.
3. **Reading-progress sync** — `/api/reading-progress` (modeled on
   listening-progress), monotone union merge, owner-stamped store so a shared
   device cannot leak one account's history into another.
4. **Account surface** — `/account` → `/settings` redirect, display name,
   email change (confirmation-time sync of `public.users.email`),
   onboarding_completed written non-fatally, middleware matcher invariant
   test.
5. **Billing hygiene** — portal resolves via `stripe_customer_id` first;
   webhook and reconciliation writers prove their writes (row-count checks).
