## Problem

Founder, invoking `/devo-go`: "Matthew 6:25-34, Philipians 4:4-7 All these
things. How to faith." With the spine given verbatim:

> "Seek ye First the Kingdom. God big promises, our crying out to Him, standing
> deeply on Faith (the movie silence), Him Being a Rock with no need for
> mediation in relationship, He is the wholeness, and all things come from Him.
> We continue to become anxious inspite of His steadyness."

Two of the most-quoted passages in Scripture, and two of the most weaponised.
"Be anxious for nothing" is routinely handed to people carrying real fear as a
rebuke. A series on these passages is worth nothing unless it can be read by
someone for whom the promise has not visibly arrived.

## What the text does

Five teaching days on the chiasm, Monday to Friday; recap Saturday; sabbath
Sunday. Every day opens with `two-minute-open-v2`.

- **Day 1 (A)** — the order in Matthew 6:33. `zēteite` is present imperative
  active, 2nd person plural: keep seeking, together. `prostethēsetai` is future
  indicative **passive** — the adding is done to you, by someone unnamed because
  in this sermon He is obvious. The passage defines "all these things" itself in
  v31 as food, drink and clothing, which closes the prosperity reading from the
  text rather than by assertion. Cross-testament: 1 Kings 3, where Solomon asks
  for an understanding heart and is given what he did not ask for — and Matthew
  6:29 then puts that same glory below a wildflower.
- **Day 2 (B)** — `merimnaō`. The misuse is named once and then dismantled on
  the Greek: Paul uses the same verb approvingly of Timothy (Phil 2:20) and of
  the church (1 Cor 12:25), and Jesus says it gently to Martha by name (Luke
  10:41). The `hēlikia` translation seam (KJV "cubit unto his stature" vs BSB "a
  single hour to his life") is shown rather than smoothed. Cross-testament:
  Exodus 16, manna that could not be hoarded.
- **Day 3 (C, pivot)** — God's silence, held open rather than resolved.
  Habakkuk files a complaint and is given a timetable with no dates and one
  assurance: lingering is not lying. `emunah` (H530) means **firmness** before it
  means belief, and its plainest occurrence is Exodus 17:12 — Moses' hands held
  steady by two other men until sunset. Cross-testament: Psalm 22:1 quoted from
  the cross in Matthew 27:46.
- **Day 4 (B′)** — `tsur` and `engys`. Immovable things are normally distant; the
  veil tore **from the top down**, from the end no one could reach. The claim is
  kept precise in both directions: no human intermediary, but one Mediator
  (1 Tim 2:5) — access _because_ of Him, not instead of one.
- **Day 5 (A′)** — `phroureō` is a garrison, and the lexicon gives it two jobs:
  keep the enemy out, **and keep the besieged city's own people from fleeing**.
  `eirēnē` is wholeness, "when all essential parts are joined together" — the
  founder's "He is the wholeness," verbatim from the lexicon. Lands on
  1 Chronicles 29:14, "for all things come of thee," and Colossians 1:17.
- **Day 6** — recap with the required Further-Your-Learning set.
- **Day 7** — sabbath. Matthew 6:34, and nothing added.

## The join the research turned up

**Deuteronomy 32:4 carries both of the founder's spine words in one verse.**
Interlinear-verified: `haṣṣūr` is H6697 ("the Rock") and `'ĕmūnāh` is H530
("faithfulness"), four words apart. The founder named "standing deeply on Faith"
and "Him Being a Rock" as separate clauses; Moses put them in one sentence. Day 3
and Day 4 therefore hinge on the same text, and Day 4 says so.

## Methodology References

Built under `M00` (`docs/methodology/M00-EUANGELION-UNIFIED-METHODOLOGY.md`) via
the `/devo-go` pipeline, which SA-029 (as amended 2026-07-22) establishes as the
standard for all prefab devotional builds.

Phase order followed: governing docs → founder clarification (AskUserQuestion) →
research fan-out for verification only → brief + source pack → **single-author
draft, days 1-7 in order** (trap #19: drafting never fans out, because per-day
writer agents produced the patchwork the founder rejected on The Harvest) →
readability gate → imagery → wiring → tracking → narration → gates → preview →
ship.

Deviation from SA-029 registered as **SA-123**: the week starts Monday, so the
sabbath moves from Day 1 to Day 7 and the recap to Day 6. All three SA-029
shapes (sabbath, A-B-C-B′-A′ chiasm, recap with Further-Your-Learning) are
preserved; only their order changes. Matthew 6:34 supplies the rationale — a week
that ends in rest is the passage's own logic.

## Imagery correction — founder review, 2026-08-24

The first series master was **rejected on biblical accuracy**, and the fault was
in the subject line rather than the generator: it contained the negative
constraint "no staff or rod." Exodus 17:9 names the rod explicitly — _"I will
stand on the top of the hill with the rod of God in mine hand"_ — so the plate
depicted the scene without the object the passage puts in Moses' hand.

Founder: _"the main image is incorrect as moses should have a staff in his
hands... Images have to be more accurate and precise. Also I would prefer the men
shown from the back, and to see the war being waged over moses shoulder. More
dynamic camera, and depiction."_

**v2 subject, regenerated:** an over-the-shoulder view from behind and above the
three men on the hilltop at Rephidim, looking steeply down into the valley.
Moses seated on the stone, both hands gripping the raised staff; Aaron and Hur
bracing an arm each; the battle between Joshua's men and Amalek visible in the
valley past Moses' shoulder; the low sun on the horizon — _"until the going down
of the sun"_ (v12).

Each element is now anchored in the text: the rod (v9), Joshua fighting below
while the three are on the hill (v10), the stone and the two supporters one on
each side (v12), and the setting sun (v12).

**Second-order benefit:** rendering all three figures from behind removed the
weakest element of v1. The hands are now closed fists around a staff shaft —
anatomically simpler and rendered cleanly — instead of the partly-merged open
hands v1 produced. SA-052 re-run at 2x zoom: five digits per hand, correct grips,
no fused or extra fingers. Square-crop check re-run and passed.

**Standing lesson for future subject lines:** a negative constraint written for
composition ("no staff") can silently delete a load-bearing textual fact. Check
every exclusion against the passage before generating.
