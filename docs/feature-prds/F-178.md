## Problem

Founder, overnight 2026-08-29: _"I need a comparison of all the attributes of God
Jesus and The Holy Spirit- and then the Hebrew names of God. (Biblical hebrew) be
comprehensive imagine this is for someone who only has heard the words God and
Jesus and has no idea for real what the faith is. Hold our hands through a unique
innovative and experimental experiential journey... as well as what the Bible is
(Gods Story) story tells why Jesus and what is salvation... This is not a
devotional but a full on intro to God."_

Everything the site had assumed a reader already inside the faith. There was no
door for someone standing entirely outside it.

Also directed the same night, to be done first: **make Seeking Help GA the last
thing on the homepage.**

## Solution

One route, `/who-is-god`, that wears none of the app chrome and reads as its own
world. Eight numbered chapters, each with a founder-approved plate:

1. Start here, knowing nothing — states the shape of what follows, and that you
   may stop at any point.
2. Not a force. Someone. — the claim is personhood, and speech is the evidence.
3. A story in four movements — good, broken, rescued, restored.
4. The names — 15 Hebrew names, script + transliteration + gloss + the moment
   each was given, as an accessible disclosure list.
5. One God, Father Son and Spirit — the comparison, below.
6. Why Jesus, and not just good advice.
7. What salvation actually is.
8. What you could do next — three steps, ordered by how much they ask.

**The comparison** is a two-view control, because the doctrine is two claims held
together and a single table can only argue one of them. _What is the same_ is a
7×3 matrix — eternal, creator, all-knowing, everywhere present, holy, gives life,
called God — with a verse in every cell, which is the argument for one God.
_What is different_ is three cards on sending and being sent, closing on the
baptism where all three act at once.

**The film.** The hero is a scroll-scrubbed video: scroll position drives
`video.currentTime` rather than the video playing itself. Seeded from the
existing `genesis-two-stories-of-creation` plate so the motion is the brand's own
riso print — cobalt sea, ploughed land, an amber line of light at the horizon —
and not a stock look.

## Constraints honoured

- **Scripture is corpus-verbatim.** All 92 verses extracted by script from
  `public/bibles/BSB`. None typed from memory.
- **Stills are not generated.** `CLAUDE.md` forbids Claude Code generating
  imagery here; `public/images/library/` is gitignored and absent from the tree.
  All seven plates are founder-approved series plates already on disk.
- **Hebrew is never unpaired with transliteration** (devo-go guardrail).
- **≤1,000 ComfyUI credits.** One render, ~319.

## The scrub only works because the film is fetched into a blob

Cloudflare Workers' asset handler does not answer HTTP Range requests for this
bundle: `Range: bytes=0-1023` returns `200` with the whole 4.5 MB and no
`Accept-Ranges`. Chrome will not seek a progressive video it cannot range-
request, so `video.seekable` was an empty `[0,0]` range and every write to
`currentTime` silently read back `0`. The scrub was fully implemented and did
nothing — the hero was a still first frame.

`ScrubbedFilm` therefore downloads the file once with `fetch`, creates an object
URL, and points the element at that. The media is then local, so seeking is
instant and exact. Same bytes, smoother result. Measured on the deployed build:
`seekable.end(0)` 0 → 5.042, and scroll drives `currentTime` 0 → 4.149 → 5.037.

If the fetch fails the element falls back to the network path, which still gives
a poster and a first frame.

## Known gaps

- `design-system/typography.css:71` declares `@font-face` for SBL Hebrew at
  `/fonts/SBLHebrew.woff2`, and **that file is not in `public/fonts/`**. Hebrew
  therefore renders in the Times New Roman fallback. It is legible and correctly
  vocalised, but it is not the intended face. Dropping the woff2 in fixes it with
  no code change.
- The site-wide cookie notice overlays the hero's sub-copy on a first visit.
  Pre-existing component, not introduced here.
