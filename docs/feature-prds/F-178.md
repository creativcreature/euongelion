## Problem

Founder, overnight 2026-08-29: _"I need a comparison of all the attributes of God
Jesus and The Holy Spirit- and then the Hebrew names of God. (Biblical hebrew) be
comprehensive imagine this is for someone who only has heard the words God and
Jesus and has no idea for real what the faith is. Hold our hands through a unique
innovative and experimental experiential journey... as well as what the Bible is
(Gods Story) story tells why Jesus and what is salvation... This is not a
devotional but a full on intro to God."_

Everything the site had assumed a reader already inside the faith. There was no
door for someone standing entirely outside it.

Also directed the same night, to be done first: **make Seeking Help GA the last
thing on the homepage.**

## Solution

One route, `/who-is-god`, that wears none of the app chrome and reads as its own
world. Eight numbered chapters, each with a founder-approved plate:

1. Start here, knowing nothing — states the shape of what follows, and that you
   may stop at any point.
2. Not a force. Someone. — the claim is personhood, and speech is the evidence.
3. A story in four movements — good, broken, rescued, restored.
4. The names — 15 Hebrew names, script + transliteration + gloss + the moment
   each was given, as an accessible disclosure list.
5. One God, Father Son and Spirit — the comparison, below.
6. Why Jesus, and not just good advice.
7. What salvation actually is.
8. What you could do next — three steps, ordered by how much they ask.

**The comparison** is a two-view control, because the doctrine is two claims held
together and a single table can only argue one of them. _What is the same_ is a
7×3 matrix — eternal, creator, all-knowing, everywhere present, holy, gives life,
called God — with a verse in every cell, which is the argument for one God.
_What is different_ is three cards on sending and being sent, closing on the
baptism where all three act at once.

**The film.** The hero is a scroll-scrubbed video: scroll position drives
`video.currentTime` rather than the video playing itself. Seeded from the
existing `genesis-two-stories-of-creation` plate so the motion is the brand's own
riso print — cobalt sea, ploughed land, an amber line of light at the horizon —
and not a stock look.

## Constraints honoured

- **Scripture is corpus-verbatim.** All 92 verses extracted by script from
  `public/bibles/BSB`. None typed from memory.
- **Stills are not generated.** `CLAUDE.md` forbids Claude Code generating
  imagery here; `public/images/library/` is gitignored and absent from the tree.
  All seven plates are founder-approved series plates already on disk.
- **Hebrew is never unpaired with transliteration** (devo-go guardrail).
- **≤1,000 ComfyUI credits.** One render, ~319.

## The scrub only works because the film is fetched into a blob

Cloudflare Workers' asset handler does not answer HTTP Range requests for this
bundle: `Range: bytes=0-1023` returns `200` with the whole 4.5 MB and no
`Accept-Ranges`. Chrome will not seek a progressive video it cannot range-
request, so `video.seekable` was an empty `[0,0]` range and every write to
`currentTime` silently read back `0`. The scrub was fully implemented and did
nothing — the hero was a still first frame.

`ScrubbedFilm` therefore downloads the file once with `fetch`, creates an object
URL, and points the element at that. The media is then local, so seeking is
instant and exact. Same bytes, smoother result. Measured on the deployed build:
`seekable.end(0)` 0 → 5.042, and scroll drives `currentTime` 0 → 4.149 → 5.037.

If the fetch fails the element falls back to the network path, which still gives
a poster and a first frame.

## The overhaul (2026-08-30, second pass)

The first build shipped a good hero and a conventional article behind it. The
founder rejected it: _"You added one really kewl thing at the begining and then
made rhe rest of it basic"_, and _"This is a basic ass webpag and you didnt pull
ANY research I can tell."_ Both correct.

Rebuilt as **seven rooms, the seventh being the threshold**, per founder
direction. Seven is the most solidly attested number in Scripture; the scrubbed
film stops being a room and becomes the door you pass through to reach them.

**One idea drives every motion decision: the page brightens as you descend.**
`--room-light` runs 0 → 1 across the document (LightSpine), driving ground
colour, type colour, plate opacity and veil. It has exactly one dip, and
Scripture put it there — Matthew 27:45, "From the sixth hour until the ninth hour
darkness came over all the land", so Room 05 falls to 0.18 before recovering.
That is the text describing the sky and the page obeying it.

Each pattern traces to a named exemplar, cited at the file that implements it:

| Pattern                                                | Exemplar                                                         | File                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Rooms you move between, not a page you scroll          | Cartier Watches & Wonders 2026 (Immersive Garden, Awwwards SOTD) | `Room.tsx`                           |
| Sticky visual + stepper text, trigger line at 45%      | NYT _Snow Fall_ → Scrollama (Russell Samora, The Pudding)        | `StickyStepper.tsx`                  |
| Scroll-scrubbed video, not frame sequences             | Apple AirPods Pro `scroll-gallery`                               | `ScrubbedFilm.tsx`                   |
| One graphic, many states, built a row at a time        | The Pudding sticky-chart stepper                                 | `CompareStage.tsx`                   |
| Pinned item, scroll picks the state                    | Apple `scroll-group`                                             | `NameStage.tsx`                      |
| Separation by whitespace not rules; monospace readouts | Nous portal                                                      | `who-is-god.css`, `ProgressRail.tsx` |

Deliberately NOT copied: Cartier's WebGL, Web Audio score and hidden gestures.
Their grammar sells an object; ours introduces a person. A ~500KB Three.js
payload would also exclude the reader this page exists for, on the Workers free
tier at 10ms CPU.

**Measured results**

- Reading level: Flesch-Kincaid **2.74** (repo target ≤ 8.5), 8.9 words per
  sentence, zero sentences over 30 words, zero over 45 — using the exact formula
  in `scripts/check-readability.mjs`.
- Reading column centred; every `border-top` section rule removed.
- Six new plates generated via `/imagen` → Codex `image_gen`, 1536×1024 at the
  full pixel budget, installed as webp.
- Seeking Help Georgia is now a full section in Room 07 with its own plate.

## The dip, corrected

The seven-room commit claimed the spine falls in Room 05 and it did not:
`LightSpine` read `data-light` but never `data-dip`, so the page rose
monotonically while the CHANGELOG, PRD and spec all said otherwise. A half-sine
is zero at both edges and one at the middle, so the room enters at its own light
and leaves at the next room's, going dark only in between.

Measured in the Workers preview: **entering 0.74, bottoming at 0.18 mid-room,
recovering to 0.88.**

## Image loading, measured and corrected

**A correction first.** An earlier measurement of this page reported an 18-second
LCP. That was a measurement error: LCP stops updating at first _user input_, and a
programmatic scroll is not input, so the figure kept climbing as larger images
scrolled past. Measured correctly, with no scroll, **LCP is 92-456 ms and FCP is
84-336 ms**. The page was never failing Core Web Vitals.

**The real waste was different and real.** The StoryStage rendered all four
movement plates eagerly so it could cross-dissolve between them, so **2,327 KB of
plates were requested at 24 ms** for a room thousands of pixels below the fold:
`deep` 400 KB, `rescue` 718 KB, `break` 703 KB, `restore` 506 KB. Eleven `<img>`
tags served six files; `deep.webp` appeared three times.

Fixed:

- 960px derivatives (`<name>-sm.webp`) for every stage slot, which renders at
  ~416 CSS px. Average 62% smaller: the stage set went 3,657 KB to 1,382 KB.
- The story stage now renders only the plate in view and the next one. The
  cross-dissolve needs two in the DOM, not four.
- `loading="lazy"`, `decoding="async"`, `fetchPriority="low"` and explicit
  intrinsic dimensions on every plate.
- The door film's 4.3 MB fetch is deferred to first scroll or main-thread idle,
  whichever comes first. The poster is already painted; nothing needs the video
  until the reader moves.

**Measured after: plate bytes before any scroll fell from 2,327 KB to 400 KB, an
83% reduction.** Scrubbing verified intact (blob src, `seekable` 5.04,
`currentTime` tracking to 4.62).

**This reverses the planned upscale-to-2400 step.** Serving 2400x1600 would roughly
double image payload for no visible gain at these display sizes. The 2400 masters
stay in `design-sources/`; the served files get smaller, not bigger. Note also that
`next.config.ts` sets `images.unoptimized`, so srcset is not available here.

## The page is climbed, not descended

Founder direction: _"Instead of descending the page - you ascend. You start on the
bottom and work north."_ Clarified as a normal scroll: the page loads at the
bottom and the reader scrolls up.

`.wig-main` is `flex-direction: column-reverse`, so the first DOM child (the door)
renders at the visual bottom and Room 07 sits at the top. The reader lands at the
foot and climbs into full light.

**Why column-reverse rather than reversing the markup:** DOM order stays in
narrative order, so a screen reader, a crawler and the no-JS page all still read
the door then Room 01 first. Reversing the markup would have made the accessible
reading order run backwards - the same class of failure as scrolljacking, which
the research for this page flags as a WCAG 2.1 keyboard and screen-reader break.

Three things had to invert with it, each caught by measuring rather than assuming:

1. **The light spine.** Geometry still measures downward while narrative progress
   now runs up, so `within` is inverted. Before the fix the door read light 1.0 -
   full daylight at the exact moment it should be black.
2. **The out-of-range fallbacks**, since the geometrically-first element on screen
   is now the LAST room.
3. **The film.** The door sits at its geometric end, so the reader landed on the
   final frame and the last beat, with the whole sequence running backwards as
   they climbed. `--film-progress` is inverted too.

The door also declares `data-light="0"`, or the spine has no anchor at the foot of
the page and falls back to the last room's value.

Measured on the climb: **door 0.03 → 0.12 → 0.29 → 0.48 → 0.65 → Room 05 at 0.18
(the ninth hour) → 0.94 → 1.0.** Ground travels from near-black navy to cream.

## Two ways up

Someone opening this at three in the morning does not owe the page a twenty-minute
climb. The door offers a choice: **The climb** (seven rooms) or **The short way**
(the same story, straight).

Short mode keeps every room, claim, verse and attribution in the DOM and only
collapses the stepped expansions - so with JavaScript off, or for a crawler, the
page is still the complete text. Short is something a reader chooses, never a
truncation imposed on them. Room 07 stays whole in both modes, Seeking Help
Georgia included.

Measured: **42,398 px → 9,814 px, a 77% shorter page.**

## The door veil, rebalanced

Landing at the bottom means landing on the brightest frame in the film - a cream
sky. The veil had been tuned for the old darker opening and left the headline
barely legible. It now carries a band of real darkness across the middle third
where the copy sits, and the beats keep a text-shadow floor so no frame can wash
them out.

## The comparison, comprehensive

Founder asked for "a comparison of all the attributes of God Jesus and The Holy
Spirit", then, on the tension with a beginner audience: "comprehensive- but you
can tuck things away."

**Eighteen attributes**, every one said in Scripture of the Father, the Son AND
the Spirit, with a verse in all **54 cells** — each reference verified present in
`public/bibles/BSB` before being written, none quoted from memory. Seven show by
default; the other eleven sit behind a control that **names the count** ("11 more,
all said of all three"), so nothing is silently withheld from a reader who wants
the whole picture.

Added beyond the original seven: sovereign, just, merciful, faithful, unchanging,
self-existent, wise, patient, good, gracious, loving.

Covered by `__tests__/who-is-god-compare.test.tsx` — 4 tests asserting the row
count, the core split, that all 54 cells carry a real reference and text, and the
show/hide behaviour.

**A test-config finding, left alone deliberately:** `vitest.config.ts` does not set
`globals: true`, so Testing Library's automatic `afterEach` cleanup never
registers and renders accumulate across tests within a file. This affects the
whole suite, not just this page. Cleanup is called explicitly in this test file
rather than changing shared config as a side effect of a feature.

## The stage alternates sides

Hermes Agent's feature sequence alternates its image left and right down the page;
pinning it to one side is the monotony that pattern exists to avoid. Rooms 01, 03
and 05 carry the stage left, 02 and 04 right. The stage keeps the wide column
either way, so it is the same size whichever side it faces.

Three independent references converged on this: Hermes' asymmetry, Dribbble's
editorial cards overlapping their image rather than sitting beside it, and the
floating-card composition in four of six Awwwards Sites of the Day.

## The names, comprehensive

Founder asked for the Hebrew names "(Biblical hebrew) be comprehensive". The first
build shipped fifteen. Now **twenty-five**, each with script, transliteration,
gloss, and the moment it was given.

Added: El Gibbor (Isaiah 9:6), YHWH Tseva'ot (1 Samuel 1:3), YHWH Mekaddishkem
(Exodus 31:13), El Chai (Joshua 3:10), Qanna (Exodus 34:14), Tsur (Deuteronomy
32:4), Magen (Genesis 15:1), Go'ali (Job 19:25), Av (Isaiah 64:8), El Bethel
(Genesis 35:7).

**All 25 quotations verified byte-identical to `public/bibles/BSB`** by parsing the
data file and comparing against the corpus, not by inspection.

Deliberately excluded: Abba (Mark 14:36) and Attiq Yomin / Ancient of Days (Daniel
7:9). Both are **Aramaic, not Hebrew**, and the ask was Biblical Hebrew. Naming the
exclusion rather than quietly padding the count.
