## User Expectation

- CANONICAL ANGLE (founder brief, 2026-08-10): **steadfastness with God.** God has made a massive, unwavering commitment; there is nonetheless a massive gap between Him and us — and the gap is ours. The reader in view is anyone "constantly choosing their own way rather than choosing to rely on God in every single aspect of their life… They still want to be their own mini god. They're still stealing that fruit from the Garden of Eden." The required posture is deeply reflective: humanity looking at itself in the face of a God who loves Himself so deeply that He will hold it accountable — and who has also provided the way back after **we** broke the trust and the covenants.
- **Harvest-adjacent, not harvest.** The founder's Sunday-school class is in the Sower and the Wheat and the Tares (Mark vs. Matthew) and the season is still harvest, but this is not another parable week — `the-harvest` owns Matthew 13. This series takes the harvest's agricultural logic through Hosea's sowing and reaping and turns it on covenant faithfulness.
- **Seed vs. anchor.** The founder came to 1 Thessalonians 5:1–10 over the weekend and said explicitly it is _not_ the week's scripture — it is the week's sentiment. It opens Day 1 and closes Day 6 as a secondary passage and is never promoted to anchor (SA-034(3)).
- **THIRD PERSON.** The founder's sharpest constraint: the reading is written in third person / first-person plural, not second person. Direct address survives only where the form itself is address — reflection, prayer, takeaway, interactive (SA-034(2)).
- Monday start, Sunday Sabbath close. Every non-sabbath day opens with the **Two-Minute Open v2** (SA-034(1)): scripture → vocab → a short write-up about that scripture → reflection → prayer → DEEP DIVE cta.
- Definition of done, in the founder's words: he is "beyond wowed" with the content; it "feels transformative"; it "can reach even the darkest places in a person's heart."

## 10/10 Definition

- Days 1–5 deep dives on the A-B-C-B′-A′ chiasm; Day 6 Saturday recap with Further-Your-Learning; Day 7 Sunday sabbath (Lamentations 3:22–23).
- The week lands one idea from two directions: God's faithfulness rests on His own nature, so He can neither call the damage nothing (which would make Him a liar) nor abandon the people who did it (which would also make Him a liar). Judgment and mercy are one attribute facing two ways, and the cross is God being consistent with Himself at His own expense.
- All content per the /devo-go standard: corpus-verbatim scripture (allowed translations only), interlinear-verified lexicon with an explicit downgrade list, primary-source stories with folklore rejected and documented, verbatim fully-cited quotes, oEmbed + embed-block verified videos on official channels only.
- SA-032 cross-testament connection developed on every teaching day. Interpretive debates (2 Timothy 2:13 comfort-vs-warning; Hosea 6:1–3 glib-vs-genuine; Pauline authorship of the Pastorals; the Jeremiah 31:32 / Hebrews 8:9 MT-LXX divergence) presented as ranges, not settled.
- No passage printed twice in a day; no word taught twice in a day or across days (the per-day two-passage/two-word table in the brief is the mechanism).
- Validator 0/0 under the new `two-minute-open-v2` sequence AND cta-target rules; `the-harvest` still 0/0 under the legacy shape.
- devotional-editor pass to READY, including the SA-032 cross-day repetition sweep and a third-person grep across all non-exempt module prose.
