## Problem

Two problems, both found by reviewing "The Listening Plan" against the codebase
rather than executing it.

**One: the plan was stale.** It is dated 19 August, second draft, _"Nothing
built yet."_ Eight of its nine stages had shipped that same day under SA-098 and
SA-104 through SA-115 — R2, the global element, the bar, queues, the library,
the voiced Edition, the occasion picker, downloads. Executing it as written
would have rebuilt shipped work. Two of its three named continuity bugs were
already fixed: the throttle is per-track, and the finish handler's `seconds: 0`
is deliberate because it carries `ended: true` alongside.

**Two: the third bug survived by moving.** `NarrationPlayer` has saved on
`pagehide` and `visibilitychange` since it owned the audio element. SA-115
hoisted playback into `GlobalAudioHost` and the handler did not come with it.
Everything played outside the reader panel — the drawer queue, the occasion
picker, /today, the Edition — could lose up to thirty seconds of position on
tab close or on a phone being locked. Both of the host's push sites also sent a
hard-coded `listenedDelta: 0`, so that listening accumulated no total at all.

A third defect surfaced while fixing the second: `pushPosition` returned
nothing, so callers cleared their accumulator whether or not the account had
taken the write. The interval runs every 5s against a 30s throttle, which means
the listening total was discarding roughly five of every six ticks.

## What was built

**The continuity fixes.** `pushPosition` now reports whether the server write
happened, and both call sites clear the accumulator only when it did.
`GlobalAudioHost` accumulates real elapsed playback with the same
`delta > 0 && delta < 2` clamp `NarrationPlayer` uses — deliberately identical,
because two accumulators answering "was that listening?" from different
arithmetic would eventually disagree and the account would hold a number neither
could explain. The host registers `pagehide` and `visibilitychange`, guarded so
that merely loading the site cannot write a row at position 0 and count an open
as a listen.

**The breadth axis.** `formats.ts` introduces the missing noun. The system knew
two shapes of audio and modelled neither; a format declares its length band, the
activities it suits, and whether it is audio-native — the three formats that
would be poor as reading and that no restructuring of the devotional corpus
produces.

**Scripture-whole, made real.** The Office, Night and Lectio are new writing and
new recording, and `AUDIO_NATIVE_PIECES` is deliberately empty for them —
inventing pieces to close a plan item is the failure SA-098 refused and this
repeats the refusal. But `bible-365` is 365 delivered tracks running in
canonical order, so grouped by book it is long-form already: 45 consecutive
runs across 31 books, 31 of which clear 40 minutes and 26 of which land inside
the format's 40–90 band — 46.1 hours of long-form that was always there. The
occasion picker's "an hour or more" returned nothing; it now offers
whole books. Nothing was recorded to make that true — it was always there,
addressed a day at a time.

Book identity comes from `parseReference`, the site's only scripture reference
parser, and that is correctness rather than tidiness: the corpus carries
thematic headings — `Sabbath`, `Selected`, `Amos, Hosea, Micah` — that a naive
"strip the trailing numbers" split would offer a listener as books.
`lookupBookId` rejects all three, and a test pins it.

The queue is the long-form unit, not the track. Each item is still an
eleven-minute reading, so it still satisfies the per-piece ceiling, still
resumes per track, and still survives being stopped halfway. One 90-minute file
would do none of that.

## Platform Contracts

### Desktop

- The occasion picker's "an hour or more" offers whole books, shortest first,
  for commuting and working; resting still caps piece length, so it offers none.
- Leaving the site mid-track writes the position to the account, from any
  surface, not only the reader panel.
- A book queue never contains an item without audio.

### Mobile

- Backgrounding or locking the phone flushes the position through `sendBeacon`,
  which is the far more common way listening ends on a phone.
- The book list scrolls within a bounded height so it cannot bury the play
  button inside the audio sidebar.
- Every control keeps the 44px minimum target.
