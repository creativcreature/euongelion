## The constants (apply to EVERY plate, pinned or chosen)

- **Frame:** 2400 × 658 native (CSS ratio 3358/920). Generate wide, Photoshop-crop exact.
- **Inks:** strict duotone — deep cobalt/ultramarine on warm cream + crimson
  misregistration offset only. No greys; every shadow is dot density.
- **Density:** the tomb's register — dark cobalt masses ~75%+ of frame, broken by one
  act of light. The seven must feel like one press run; no airy plates in this set.
- **Subject dead center** (founder 2026-08-19), and usually its light too. Supporting
  masses fall to the margins. Overrides the preamble's off-center allowance for this set.
- **One light with a story** — breaks, spills, descends, or goes out.
- **People:** ancient Levant only — period dress, dense-dot brown skin, faces by pose.
  No modern person, object, or garment. All devo-go prompt blocks apply
  (`scripts/imagery/prompt-preamble.md`): full bleed, no bare paper, grounded, hands,
  no-AI-tells, no text.
- **Style anchor:** `public/images/site/homepage/hero/header-v2.webp` attached to every
  generation. **Composition reference:** the card's film still attached alongside.

## PINNED POOL (20 — approved concepts, not yet generated)

Each entry: scripture · gospel logic · staging · film source (film-grab URL) · still file.

### 01 · The Ram in the Thicket — Genesis 22:8, 13

Substitution: "God will provide for Himself the lamb." Moriah's summit ridge runs the
width; stayed altar small at far left; at dead center the ram caught in black thorns,
sunrise breaking exactly behind its horns — the light lives in the substitute. Crimson
on the thorn branches. — The Revenant (skull-pyramid dawn frame),
https://film-grab.com/2017/08/29/the-revenant/ · still-01.jpg

### 02 · The Ladder Out of Heaven — Genesis 28:12 · John 1:51

Heaven opened; Jesus tells Nathanael the stair is Him. Night ridge, sleeper against his
stone at center; sky torn directly above, one broad stair of light stepping straight
down to touch him, ascending figures as notches in the dots. Crimson at the tear. —
The Tree of Life (vertical flame-of-light frame),
https://film-grab.com/2012/12/17/the-tree-of-life/ · still-02.jpg

### 04 · The Road Through the Sea — Exodus 14:21–22

A way through death. Two immense curling water walls flank a cream corridor up the
center; tiny caravan at its heart; pillar of fire at the corridor's end, crimson-cored.
— Arrival (ship over misted valley),
https://film-grab.com/2019/06/28/arrival/ · still-04.jpg

### 05 · Bread Out of Heaven — Exodus 16:4 · John 6:35

Grace on the ground; "I am the bread of life." Desert floor white with manna like
frost — the ground carries the light; two stooped gatherers dark at center; low sun on
the far-left horizon streaking shadows. **FOUNDER DIRECTION PRESERVED: literal restage
of the Days of Heaven still** — same stooped figures, same low light, harvesters become
manna gatherers in period dress. (Was in the founder's first six; swapped out for 26 —
first in line for a pool expansion.) — Days of Heaven,
https://film-grab.com/2010/07/21/days-of-heaven/ · still-05.jpg

### 06 · The Goat Sent Away — Leviticus 16:21–22

Sin carried off. Dark stacked dunes, shimmering dot sky; the goat tiny at the dead
center of the ridge, mid-stride toward the right where heat-light dissolves the horizon;
camp tents barely hold the left edge. — Lawrence of Arabia (mirage emptiness),
https://film-grab.com/2013/02/10/lawrence-of-arabia/ · still-06.jpg

### 07 · The Serpent Lifted Up — Numbers 21:8–9 · John 3:14

Look and live. Pole dead center, bronze serpent coiled on it, the glow blooming at its
top (the tomb's radiance relocated to a standard); bitten camp masses dark in both
lower corners, faces lifted. Crimson traces the serpent. — Close Encounters (upturned
faces bathed in overhead light),
https://film-grab.com/2012/11/20/close-encounters-of-the-third-kind/ · still-07.jpg

### 08 · The Scarlet Cord — Joshua 2:18–21

Marked for mercy. Jericho's wall fills the entire band; one small window burns warm at
the exact center, the scarlet cord dropping from it — the only crimson on the plate. —
The Green Knight (monumental dark chamber, one glowing window),
https://film-grab.com/2023/04/04/the-green-knight/ · still-08.jpg

### 09 · Three Days in the Deep — Jonah 1:17 · Matthew 12:40

The sign of Jonah. The great fish a vast dark mass in the lower band; surface glowing
with dawn above; Jonah rising small in a column of light and breath at center; hard
cream waterline seam. — Life of Pi (luminous whale beneath the raft at night),
https://film-grab.com/2021/01/05/life-of-pi/ · still-09.jpg

### 11 · One Star Over the Ridge — Matthew 2:9–10

The light arrives for the nations. Magi caravan in silhouette along a low ridge,
approaching from the right; one oversized star at dead center, its single ray falling
to the stable lamp directly beneath on the horizon; sky in graded dots. — The Seventh
Seal (closing danse macabre ridge),
https://film-grab.com/2013/02/26/the-seventh-seal/ · still-11.jpg

### 13 · The Shepherd Who Goes Out — Luke 15:4–5 · John 10:11

The seeking God. Night slope across the band; fold's gate lamp low left with the
ninety-nine as a pale dotted mass; at the center of the slope the shepherd descends
against the last light, the one across his shoulders — the only pale shape outside the
fold. — Brokeback Mountain (moonlit flock on dark mountainside),
https://film-grab.com/2013/05/22/brokeback-mountain/ · still-13.jpg

### 14 · Seen From Far Off — Luke 15:20

The gospel in one verb: the father runs. One straight road owns the band, vanishing to

mid-run, robe streaming with crimson ghosting its edge; the light where they will meet.
— Paris, Texas (walker in vast emptiness),
https://film-grab.com/2010/06/19/paris-texas/ · still-14.jpg

### 15 · The Grain That Dies — John 12:24

center, roots reaching down; one pale blade risen through the soil line into a thin
strip of dawn along the top. — Stalker (moss-and-loam Zone textures),
https://film-grab.com/2012/07/31/stalker/ · still-15.jpg

### 16 · The Cup and the Bread — Luke 22:19–20

The covenant, served. The table runs the full width in darkness; one candle at center;

chiaroscuro), https://film-grab.com/2010/07/08/barry-lyndon/ · still-16.jpg

### 17 · The Cup Accepted — Luke 22:42–44

https://film-grab.com/2025/06/04/andrei-rublev/ · still-17.jpg

### 18 · Darkness at Noon — Luke 23:44–45

Melancholia (final frame: the planet over three figures),
https://film-grab.com/2016/01/29/melancholia/ · still-18.jpg

### 19 · The Father's View — Matthew 27:50–51

Straight down at the moment of death. Overhead plan: the cross a dark cruciform on pale

The Passion of the Christ (overhead Golgotha crane),
https://film-grab.com/2016/08/01/the-passion-of-the-christ/ · still-19.jpg

### 20 · The Veil, Torn — Matthew 27:51

The way in, opened from God's side. The veil IS the frame — woven bands edge to edge;

(massed banner fabric filling the frame),
https://film-grab.com/2017/02/25/kagemusha/ · still-20.jpg

### 21 · Breakfast on the Shore — John 21:9–12

Resurrection turned toward restoration. Dawn bands the horizon, sun rising dead center;

https://film-grab.com/2020/08/25/the-red-turtle/ · still-21.jpg

### 23 · The Stoning of Stephen — Acts 7:55–60

The first witness dies like his Lord and sees the Son of Man STANDING. Stephen kneels

### 24 · The Sower and the Weeds — Matthew 13:3–30

SA-032/SA-052 precedent. — A Hidden Life (mid-stroke scything against the Dolomites),
https://film-grab.com/2020/09/07/a-hidden-life/ · still-24.jpg
