### Base style prompt — per-format adaptation notes

Base (from run instructions Task 6): vintage editorial duotone riso/screen-print; cream `#F2EBD8` paper, ultramarine `#1B3FAE` ink majority, crimson `#8B1F2E` spot accent used sparingly; heavy Ben-Day halftone in all tones (no grays — shadow is dot density); offset-print grain, slight misregistration; **no text** (Hebrew/Greek allowed as content); no photographs.

- **Wide 21:9 (headers):** high horizon, single subject off-center RIGHT, cream negative space held on the LEFT third for headline overlay.
- **Card 4:3:** subject centered-left, breathing room at top; medium detail.
- **Thumbnail square 1:1:** one subject dead-center, bolder/simplified forms, higher ink coverage — must read at 120 px.
- **Empty-state object:** exactly ONE devotional object on a plain cream field, centered, generous margins, no scene, no ground line beyond a hint of shadow-dots.
- **Animated (test only):** loop grain/dot-lattice shimmer; subject itself does not move.
