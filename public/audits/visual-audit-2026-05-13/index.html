§01 · Executive verdict

The site is almost there. Five moves close the gap.

A Steve Jobs reduction. The hard part — voice, content discipline, a typestack worth winning Site of the Day — is done. The remaining gap is publication craft and one missing primitive (share).

Euangelion has the rare problem of being too close to great. The voice is rare. The content discipline is rare. The Riso/halftone register is genuinely differentiated. And yet — by the awwwards-tier bar the founder has asked for, the site is roughly 80% there. The remaining 20% is craft you can do in three sprints, plus the social spine the product currently has none of.

Read this section if you only read one. The other eight back it up.

What is working
  1. A real editorial voice. “Daily bread for the cluttered, hungry soul” is on-tone. Section copy reads like an editor wrote it, not a marketer. Keep this. Defend it.
  2. A typestack people would cite. Instrument Serif + Industry Test is the right pair. Both are restrained, both have personality, both are free. The pair alone gets you on awwwards’ shortlist if the rest holds.
  3. A coherent visual register. After the round 18 image purge, every surface reads as printed broadsheet. The 9 photographs that broke the spell are gone; the 24 compliant linocuts already pulling weight are stronger for it.
  4. No engagement bait. No streaks, no badges, no “3-day challenge.” In the contemplative-Christian category, this is a moat. Lectio 365 won this register; Hallow burned it. Euangelion is on the right side.
What’s holding it back
  1. Publication furniture is missing. HighNo folios, no edition numbers, no datelines at the top of reading. The 2026 NYT Magazine redesign moved page furniture above the title for exactly this register. We don’t even have it.
  2. One token lies. Critical--color-gold resolves to #1f2a8d (cobalt). Every reference to “gold” in docs is wrong. Drop caps and focus rings inherit a broken assumption. Pick one and rewrite the cascade.
  3. No share primitive. CriticalSubstack’s entire growth engine is Notes/Restack and per-post OG cards. Euangelion has neither dynamic OG images per devotional nor an in-app share affordance. The whole social plan is downstream of this missing API.
  4. Reading chrome doesn’t fade. HighApple, Read.cv, The Pudding — every winner clears chrome once the reader starts scrolling. We stay chrome-up. The page should become the page.
  5. Day-nav blocks the reader on mobile. HighThe day picker eats above-the-fold real estate on the devotional page. Push it to a thumb-zone pill that hides on scroll-down, returns on scroll-up.

Top five moves · in order

Do these five things and Euangelion ships from “live” to Site-of-the-Day-ready.

  1. Resolve the gold/cobalt token cascade.Rename --color-gold or restore the amber hex. Audit every doc that says “gold.” Effort: S.
  2. Add publication furniture: folios, datelines, edition labels.Above every devotional: EUANGELION · VOL I · DAY 047 · 13 MAY 2026 in Industry small caps. Mirrors NYT Mag 2026. Effort: S.
  3. Ship the share primitive.Per-devotional dynamic OG image API + per-devotional shareable short URL + native share-sheet button. Without this, no distribution plan is viable. Effort: M.
  4. Ship the verse card.The atomic unit of devotional virality (YouVersion proved it). /api/verse-card?ref=Mark+1:3&trans=BSB renders a branded single-verse PNG. Effort: M.
  5. Move day-nav off-fold + clear chrome on scroll.Floating bottom-edge thumb pill on mobile; the masthead fades past 80vh. Reader becomes the reader. Effort: M.

§04 · Craft polish — fifty-six details

Where Steve Jobs would stop, lean in, and ask “what about this?”

A close read of the codebase against awwwards-tier expectations. Each item is shippable; effort is marked S/M/L. The Top 15 at the bottom is what to ship first.

Typography (1–15)

  1. Drop cap rests on ::first-letter.High::first-letter breaks on punctuation, quotes, em-dashes. Replace with a real <span class="dropcap"> wrapper inserted by a tiny React component. Same visual; predictable across content. Effort: S
  2. No optical tracking ramp on Instrument Serif display.MediumDisplay flat at -0.02em. At ≥48px the counters in a, e, s close up. Stepped: -0.025em @ 96–160px, -0.015em @ 48–80px, 0 @ body. Add font-optical-sizing: auto on html. Effort: S
  3. No true small caps anywhere.Hightext-transform: uppercase on section headers is 100% the wrong move for a sacred-minimalist register. Use font-variant-caps: all-small-caps on Industry Test labels. Effort: S
  4. Hanging punctuation declared, not enforced.hanging-punctuation: first last is Safari-only. Add a .punct-pull span around opening quotes in Scripture for Chrome/Firefox. Effort: S
  5. Oldstyle vs lining numerals inverted on day numerals.Body uses oldstyle (correct). .day-number at 160px hero also inherits oldstyle — “Day 7” gets a descending 7. Set lining-nums tabular-nums on architectural numerals. Effort: S
  6. Two pull-quote versions exist..pull-quote (legacy, dumb quotes, 50% gold) and .pull-quote-enhanced (10%, smart quotes). Pick the enhanced. Delete the legacy. Effort: S
  7. No alternate ampersand.HighInstrument Serif has a beautiful salt 1 ampersand. A single .amp utility — font-feature-settings: "salt" 1; font-style: italic — buys editorial credibility. Effort: S
  8. No measure cap on reader body.680px is set, but module renderers don’t all apply max-w-prose. On wide zooms, lines drift past 75ch. Effort: S
  9. Baseline grid declared but unenforced..baseline-grid exists; nothing uses it. Apply to <article> in the reader. Every spacing should be a multiple of 8px from one --baseline token. Effort: M
  10. Hebrew works — Greek doesn’t.No SBL Hebrew or SBL/GFS Greek .woff2 in /public/fonts/. Hebrew falls to system fallback. Ship SBL Hebrew + GFS Didot Polytonic. font-display: optional. Effort: S
  11. Transliteration lacks macrons/underdots.Currently italic Latin only. Use proper Unicode (ā, ḥ, š, ō) — never “sh” or “aa.” Show macrons on stressed Greek. Effort: S — but a content pass
  12. Italic-as-syntax is undefined.Italic is doing four jobs (original language, book titles, emphasis, MixedHeadline flow). Codify: italic = original-language layer; emphasis = small caps + tracking; titles = roman in <cite>. Effort: M (content audit)
  13. No footnote / margin-sidenote system.HighTufte-style margin sidenotes on desktop, numbered footnotes on mobile. One CSS-only component, huge editorial gain — perfect for theology grounding. Effort: M
  14. No text-wrap: balance / pretty.Free wins. Add balance to all h1/h2/h3, pretty to .prose p. Effort: S
  15. Wordmark doesn’t scale up.Current masthead serif sits at ~4–6rem. Push to 8–12rem in at least one state for the “type-only awwwards cover” move. Effort: S

Color (16–20)

  1. Duotone broken by greys.HighText hierarchy uses rgba(scroll, 0.7/0.5/0.4) — mathematically grey. Newspapers don’t use opacity; they use ink density. Replace tertiary text with a true second ink (desaturated cobalt or warm grey via oklch). Effort: M
  2. --color-gold is cobalt.CriticalResolve the naming/value conflict. Audit every reference to “gold” in docs (MEMORY.md, CLAUDE.md, brand-voice). Effort: S
  3. Crimson accent isn’t actually crimson.--color-burgundy: #8e3f3f is brown-red, not Riso crimson. Reserve burgundy for blood/Communion content; add a true crimson (#c4192e / #dc2626) for editorial moments. Effort: S
  4. Dark mode is inverted, not re-cast.Real editorial dark mode cools the warm cream and warms the cobalt slightly (cobalt looks navy on black). Swap via oklch(), not RGB. Effort: M
  5. Tertiary text fails WCAG AA at small sizes.rgba(scroll, 0.5) measures ~3.8:1 on tehom. Lift to 0.65 or restrict tertiary to ≥18px. Effort: S

Layout & rhythm (21–25)

  1. Page furniture missing.CriticalNo folios, no edition labels, no datelines, no continued-on marks. For a printed-newspaper aesthetic, non-negotiable. Top: EUANGELION · VOL.I · NO.[day] · [date] in Industry small caps. Bottom: — end of reading — ornament. Effort: S
  2. Column rules too pale..columns-prose uses 1px solid var(--color-border). Make it 0.5px solid currentColor at 25% opacity so it prints. Effort: S
  3. Section pacing is uniform.Real editorial design compresses fast (TOC, credits) then opens dramatically (lead). Codify three tokens: --rhythm-compressed: 16px, --rhythm-default: 32px, --rhythm-breath: 96px. Effort: M
  4. Marginalia layer unused.High.annotation style exists, nothing uses it. Reserve the desktop right margin for cross-references, liturgical date, original-language notes. The single biggest editorial differentiator vs YouVersion. Effort: M
  5. Kicker/dek/colophon hierarchy missing.Most pages: title → body. Editorial: kicker → dek (italic lead) → body → colophon (closing credit + edition tag). CSS exists; wire it through. Effort: M

Motion & interaction (26–32)

  1. Reading chrome doesn’t fade.HighHeader stays sticky during reading. useScroll → past 80vh, fade mast to 0 over 200ms; reverse on scroll-up. Effort: M
  2. No page transition between days.Crossfade + y: 16→0, 240ms cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1) on day-to-day. Reuse existing framer-motion infrastructure. Effort: S
  3. Link hover has no microinteraction.Body links: 1px underline thickens to 2px on hover, 100ms tween. Right now flat underline. Effort: S
  4. No cursor treatment.On desktop, hover an artwork or pullquote → swap cursor to a small SVG (gold fleuron or italic ampersand). One CSS line. Distinctive. Effort: S
  5. Generic loading state.app/loading.tsx should be a designed page, not a spinner. Pulsing ornament + “Loading the reading…” in Industry Book. Effort: S
  6. No haptic feedback on iOS PWA.navigator.vibrate(10) on Mark Read / Day Done. Subtle, ritual-reinforcing. Effort: S
  7. Touch targets unaudited.Bottom nav, day-nav, “next module” arrow — measure against 44pt minimum. Founder already flagged day pagination. Effort: S

Mobile reading (33–36)

  1. Masthead doesn’t reflow.At 375px the masthead should compress to “E.” monogram + dateline, not shrink the wordmark. Effort: S
  2. Day-nav blocks reading.CriticalFounder-flagged. Thumb-zone bottom-edge pill: ← Day 6 · 7 · Day 8 →, floats above iOS safe-area, 44pt tall, hides on scroll-down. Effort: M
  3. No reading-progress rule.1px gold (or crimson) rule at top of viewport that fills as you scroll. ScrollProgress.tsx exists — verify wiring. Effort: S
  4. No brightness/sepia toggle in reader.Tri-toggle: cream / sepia / dark. Three classes on <html>, three palettes. Night-reader benefits enormously. Effort: M

Content as design (37–40)

  1. No author colophon.Every devotional ends with a 4-line colophon: writer, editor, date composed, scripture translation. Treat like a movie credit. Effort: S
  2. No “About this passage” inline.Collapsible note above scripture: book, author, date written, audience. Likely already in content/. Effort: M
  3. No cross-reference marginalia.When a devotional cites another verse, show it in the right margin (desktop) or behind long-press (mobile). Don’t make the reader leave. Effort: M
  4. No liturgical calendar context.“Today is the 4th Sunday of Easter” in the dateline. One JSON field on the devotional; renders as overline. Effort: S

Performance + accessibility + SEO (41–56)

  1. Industry Test is .otf.Convert to .woff2 with fonttools subset. ~80KB saved per page. Effort: S
  2. font-display: swap missing.Only set on (unused) Google Fonts imports. Add to every @font-face in globals.css. Effort: S
  3. LCP candidate flagged.Verify homepage hero image has priority + sizes="100vw". Effort: S
  4. CLS risk on devotional artwork.Verify every <Image> in DevotionalArtwork.tsx has intrinsic width/height. Effort: S

  6. No skip-link.<a href="#main" className="sr-only focus:not-sr-only">Skip to reading</a> as first child of <body>. Effort: S
  7. Focus rings inconsistent.Currently outline: 2px solid var(--color-gold) — but gold is cobalt. Audit. Effort: S
  8. prefers-reduced-motion partial.Verify EditorialMotionSystem.tsx bails out on reduced-motion. Effort: S
  9. Heading hierarchy unaudited.Devotional reader: spot-check h1→h2→h3 with no skips. Effort: S
  10. No per-article OG image.Critical/devotional/[slug]/opengraph-image.tsx generated per day with day number + scripture reference. This is half the share story. Effort: M
  11. No Article JSON-LD per devotional.Add Article schema with author, datePublished, mainEntityOfPage, isBasedOn (scripture reference). Effort: S
  12. No CollectionPage JSON-LD per series.List days as hasPart. Effort: S
  13. BreadcrumbList JSON-LD missing.Breadcrumbs.tsx exists; verify it emits structured data. Effort: S
  14. FAQ JSON-LD on homepage.Homepage already has FAQ_ITEMS — wrap in FAQPage schema. (Already shipped per round 18 — verify.) Effort: verified

§06 · Shareability, today

The product is silent in every group chat it could be in.

The honest read: nothing about Euangelion today is built for the iMessage → friend forward → friend opens flow. Substack’s entire growth engine is downstream of that flow. So is YouVersion’s 600M-install moat.

What’s missing
  1. No dynamic OG image per devotional.Only root OG image. Every shared URL unfurls identically. Substack auto-generates a card per post; we don’t.
  2. No iMessage / WhatsApp square card.Emit both 1200×630 (X/web) and 1200×1200 (iMessage). Most reading apps miss this.
  3. No verse card export.YouVersion ships “Verse Images” — pick a background, overlay a verse, share to OS sheet. It is the atomic unit of devotional virality.
  4. No native share-sheet button on a devotional.navigator.share({title, text, url}) is one line. Should be inline after the Scripture module.
  5. No “Sarah sent you this” deep-link.Gift-link analog. Names the sender on a custom landing, offers the next 7 days, converts visitors to readers at materially higher rates (NYT, Atlantic, Hearst data).
  6. No RSS feed.30-min ship. Powers cross-post to Substack/Beehiiv via importers and lets Apple News auto-ingest.
  7. No referral counter.Beehiiv’s data: +17% subscriber growth on average from referral milestones. Build optional, opt-in.
  8. No “Restack” equivalent in-app.Substack’s viral primitive is one user signal-boosting another. Without an analog, Euangelion has no in-network growth loop.
What’s already strong
  1. Per-page metadata.Title + description per devotional were fixed in R11/R18. The skeleton is in place; the image layer is missing.
  2. Schema.org JSON-LD scaffold.WebSite + Organization + FAQPage already emit. Article + BreadcrumbList + CollectionPage are easy follow-ons.
  3. llms.txt + per-bot robots.txt.AI distribution is opted-in cleanly. We are findable by GPT, Claude, Perplexity.
  4. No paywall.Euangelion has the rare advantage that the content is free. So a “gift link” here doesn’t bypass anything — it carries a sender’s name.

The two primitives that unlock everything

Dynamic OG image API + verse-card generator. Build these two first.

Every other distribution lever — Substack cross-post, Reels, Pinterest, Threads, iMessage forwards, gift links — pulls from these two endpoints. They are infrastructure for content, not content. One sprint each.

§08 · Roadmap to ship — three sprints + an innovation sprint

What to build, in order, to move from live to worth opening every day.

Each sprint is sized at ~2 weeks for a solo founder. Innovation sprint is parallel and optional.

Sprint A · Polish

Editorial craft pass.

Resolve the gold/cobalt token cascade. Real <span class="dropcap">. Optical tracking ramp. Real small caps. Alternate ampersand. Folio + dateline + edition above each devotional. text-wrap: balance/pretty everywhere. .otf → .woff2.

Effort: ~10 days · Risk: low · Unlock: site clears the awwwards visual bar

Sprint B · Reader

Reading experience finish.

Mobile day-nav thumb pill. Reading-chrome fade past 80vh. Cream / sepia / dark tri-toggle. Tufte margin sidenotes. Crossfade page transition between days. Skip-link + focus-ring audit. Per-module measure cap.

Effort: ~10 days · Risk: low-med · Unlock: the reader becomes the reader

Sprint C · Share

Distribution primitives.

Dynamic OG image API (per devotional). Dual-ratio OG emission. Per-day short URL. Verse Card Generator. Native share-sheet inline. RSS feed. Article + BreadcrumbList + CollectionPage JSON-LD per surface. “Sarah sent you this” gift-link landing.

Effort: ~14 days · Risk: med · Unlock: every URL pasted into iMessage becomes an ad

Sprint D · Innovation (parallel)

Three demos in two weeks.

Verse card export (lives downstream of Sprint C). Today-in-the-church-year sidebar. Translate-this-verse inline (BSB / KJV / Greek). These three demo “innovation” without adding accounts or social.

Effort: ~10 days · Risk: low · Unlock: a story to tell about why Euangelion is novel

Sprint E · Audio

Voice as content.

Read-aloud (Web Speech API + ambient pad). Weekly podcast cut from seven daily readings. Audiogram pipeline (ffmpeg on Workers). Submit to Apple Podcasts + Spotify.

Effort: ~14 days · Risk: med-high · Unlock: a second discovery channel

Sprint F · Print

The mailed broadsheet.

Folded 4-page Riso of the week’s readings, $5/mo. Print-shop relationship + fulfillment. Slow, expensive, cult-forming. Defer until product-market fit is solid.

Effort: ~30+ days · Risk: high · Unlock: a physical artifact that people frame

If you only do two sprints

Sprint A and Sprint C. The visual bar plus the share spine.

Sprint A makes Euangelion look like an awwwards site. Sprint C makes it spread like one. Sprint B is the most important, but you can hold it for a follow-on once readers exist.

§09 · Decision queue

Questions only the founder can answer. Each unblocks a sprint.

Answers go in docs/MASTER-LOG.md and inform the production-decisions registry.

  1. Gold or cobalt?The brand has drifted to cobalt as the dominant ink. Should we retire the “gold” token name entirely (rename to --color-cobalt) or restore a real amber-gold hex for accents (drop caps, focus rings, pulled quotes)? Unblocks Sprint A finding #1.
  2. Crimson position.Keep burgundy #8e3f3f exclusively for Communion/blood content, add a Riso crimson #c4192e for editorial accents — agreed? Unblocks Sprint A.
  3. Substack mirror — yes or no?Cross-post weekly digest to euangelion.substack.com for the Restack loop. The decision is whether you’re comfortable being a Substack writer in parallel to running Euangelion. Unblocks the distribution playbook.
  4. Share defaults.When a reader shares a devotional, what does the default tweet/text say? Suggested: “Today’s reading: [TITLE]. From Euangelion.” + URL + verse card image. Unblocks Sprint C.
  5. Founder voice — who narrates?Read-aloud audio quality depends on a single voice. Yours, a partner’s, or a paid VO actor? Unblocks Sprint E.
  6. Newsletter ESP.Substack, Beehiiv, or Ghost for the weekly digest? Substack maximizes restack-loop; Beehiiv maximizes referral-loop; Ghost maximizes design-control. Pick one. Unblocks Sprint C/D.
  7. Marginalia: ship or defer?Margin sidenotes are the single biggest editorial differentiator — but only if there’s margin content to write. Author has to write per-day marginalia or accept it’s feature-by-feature opt-in. Unblocks Sprint B.
  8. Liturgical calendar — Western or shared?If we add “today in the church year,” which calendar? Western Christian, Eastern, BCP, RCL? Unblocks Innovation #2.
  9. Restack / Echo feed — accounts required?The strongest viral primitive (in-app re-share with reflection) requires user accounts. Today the product is account-optional. Trade-off worth discussing. Unblocks Sprint C/D.
  10. Mailed broadsheet — bet or future?$5/mo cult product or expensive distraction? Decision can wait, but the print pipeline runs ~12 weeks from yes to first mailed artifact, so know early. Unblocks Sprint F.
